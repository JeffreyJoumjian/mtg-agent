# Inquisitor Greyfax — Esper Tap/Untap

Commander: Inquisitor Greyfax (WUB)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Rhystic Study · Smothering Tithe · Cyclonic Rift

> Authoritative current list. Edit alongside STATUS.md. See ../README.md.
>
> **Gameplan:** Tap opponents' creatures, get paid for every tap, untap and do it again, then
> convert "tapped" into "dead". Greyfax is the engine in the command zone (`{1}, {T}: Tap target
> creature an opponent controls. Investigate.`) and her team-wide vigilance means your creatures
> attack *and* stay untapped as Opposition fuel. Unwinding Clock is the deck's Seedborn Muse —
> it untaps your artifacts during **each** other player's untap step, and the tappers are
> artifacts, so Icy Manipulator / Hylda's Crown / Staff of Domination fire four times per round
> instead of once. Every one of those taps triggers Hylda, Verity Circle, Sharae and Gideon's
> Avenger. Their board ends up tapped and shrinking under the assassins; yours attacks without
> ever opening up. See research/gameplan.md.
