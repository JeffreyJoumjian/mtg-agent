Parallel list: `DECK-SPELLSLINGER.md`. `DECK.md` stays the live deck while the pilot keeps

## 1. The brief, in the pilot's words

> "treat it more of a spellslinger deck and voltron second … constantly casting spells and copying
> them and clearing out enemy boards and healths at the same time … cards that allow us to draw
> whenever we cast something … just enough equipment to protect the commander and give it
> lifelink, indestructible, hexproof/protection and the rest is constant win conditions or things
> to help you draw into the win conditions, which maybe we need 10 of."

Hard constraints stated: **Hexing Squelcher and Conqueror's Flail stay in both lists; The Ozolith
stays**; add a cheap way to make the artifacts (or at least the commander) indestructible.

Play report that prompted it (2026-09-06, `DECK.md`): "struggles with draw sometimes and/or having
enough finishers to get through without commander damage."

## 7. Rules verified for this build (CR 2026-08-07, dispatched to `mtg-rules-expert`)

- **Welding Jar targets the commander** — an artifact creature satisfies "target artifact"
  (CR 205.2b). Regeneration (CR 701.19a) replaces the *next destruction this turn*: "destroy"
  effects, lethal damage and deathtouch (CR 701.8b, 704.5g–h). It does **not** stop exile,
  sacrifice, bounce, toughness ≤ 0 (CR 704.5f) or the legend rule. Side effect: she is tapped and
  removed from combat. Activate with the destroy effect on the stack (CR 117.1b); the shield is
  made on resolution, so a countered activation gives nothing.
- **Tyrite Sanctum is a two-step permanent indestructible.** The God type from the first ability
  has no duration, so it lasts until she leaves the battlefield (CR 611.2a; a new object on
  recast, CR 400.7). The second ability's indestructible *counter* is a keyword counter
  (CR 122.1b) with the same coverage boundary as regeneration (CR 702.12b, 704.5f).
- **The Ozolith carries every kind of counter**, keyword counters included: the trigger puts "the
  same number of each kind of counter" on The Ozolith (CR 122.8) and moves them all back at
  beginning of combat (CR 122.5). With the indestructible counter on her she leaves only by
  sacrifice, exile, bounce, −X/−X or the legend rule — all of which still trigger The Ozolith.
- **Delayed Blast Fireball reads the zone it was cast from.** Wanda's Vision, Arcane
  Bombardment copies and its own foretell all cast from exile (CR 707.12 for the copies; ledger
  2026-09-06). Cosmic Cube would not have — it casts from the library.
- **Jaya's Immolating Inferno** checks for a legendary creature or planeswalker as you cast it —
  seven in the list.
