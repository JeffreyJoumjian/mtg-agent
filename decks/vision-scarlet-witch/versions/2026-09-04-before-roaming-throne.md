# The Vision and Scarlet Witch — Mono-Red Spellslinger Voltron

Commander: The Vision and Scarlet Witch (mono-red)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Ancient Tomb · Jeska's Will · The One Ring

> Authoritative current list. Edit alongside STATUS.md. See ../README.md.
> Research: research/decisions.md · pilot notes: research/gameplan.md.
>
> **Gameplan:** every spell you cast refunds {R} and puts a +1/+1 counter on a 3/3 flier. So the
> deck is built out of one-mana cantrips that replace themselves (Crash Through, Expedite, Crimson
> Wisps, Might of the Meek…) — each one is mana-neutral, draws a card, grows the commander and
> fires every burn trigger on the board (Guttersnipe, Firebrand Archer, Kessig Flamebreather,
> Coruscation Mage, Thermo-Alchemist, Fiery Inscription, Longshot). Bank the surplus: Leyline
> Tyrant, Electro and Ashling stop red mana from emptying, so rituals and refunds pile up across
> turns and Braid of Fire becomes a growing deposit. Spend the bank on a storm turn — Grapeshot,
> Aetherflux Reservoir, Crackle with Power — or on a suited-up commander swing with Embercleave and
> Bulk Up, and Kediss turns one connected hit into damage to every opponent.
>
> **Protection is a role, not a card.** Hexproof (Champion's Helm, Swiftfoot Boots), protection
> from four colours (Commander's Plate), indestructible (Mithril Coat, Tyrite Sanctum), redirects
> (Deflecting Swat, Bolt Bend), uncounterable (Hexing Squelcher, Cavern of Souls), no spells on
> your turn (Conqueror's Flail), and The Ozolith so the counters survive a death. Nothing here
> covers edicts and exile wraths — the answer to those is a cheap recast plus the Ozolith bank.

**Bracket 3 compliance:** no mass land denial, no extra turns, no two-card infinite. Underworld
Breach (37% of the field) was left out on purpose — Breach + Grapeshot + any ritual is the loop
that makes this archetype a Bracket 4 deck. Reiterate is out for the same Mana Geyser reason as in
the Scarlet Witch list.

**Rules the deck runs on (verified against CR 2026-08-07, see the deck-brain ledger):**

- The commander's trigger is **not a mana ability** (CR 605.5a): it uses the stack and the {R}
  arrives before the spell resolves. It can pay for your *next* spell, never the one that made it.
- Under Leyline Tyrant, Electro or Ashling that {R} **never empties** — it carries across turns
  until spent (CR 106.4, 500.5). Announce the pool every time you pass priority (CR 106.4b).
- **Cost reducers can't touch a one-pip cantrip.** Ruby Medallion and The Fire Crystal reduce only
  generic mana (CR 601.2f); a {R} spell is already at its floor. The commander's refund is the
  only "discount" the cantrips get, and it makes them free.
- The Ozolith catches the counters when the commander dies or goes to the command zone
  (CR 603.6c, 903.9a, 122.8) and moves them back onto the recast commander at your next beginning
  of combat — recast **precombat**.
- Roaming Throne naming **Hero** makes her trigger twice per spell — {R}{R} and two counters — and
  doubles Wiccan's impulse. It must be on the battlefield **before** the spell is cast (doublers
  count at trigger time, CR 603.2d); Kediss is a Lizard and is not covered.
- Commander's Plate on a mono-red commander is protection from **white, blue, black and green**:
  it stops targeted removal, blocks and damage from those colours, and does nothing against wraths,
  edicts, or red and colorless sources (CR 702.16).
