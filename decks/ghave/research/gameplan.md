# Ghave, Guru of Spores — Pilot's Gameplan

## 0. The one-paragraph version

**Ghave is a bank. Counters go in, Saprolings come out.** He arrives with five +1/+1 counters, and
each one becomes a 1/1 Saproling for {1}, at instant speed. Every creature you sacrifice to him ({1})
puts a counter back. You don't need to draw token makers to have a board: the commander *is* one.
The 99 multiplies that (white and green doublers), turns every death into damage (Blood Artist,
Zulaport, Grave Pact), and keeps Ghave fed with more counters.

## 1. The curve you actually have

| | MV1 | MV2 | MV3 | MV4 | MV5 | MV6+ | avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nonland incl. Ghave (65) | 10 | 19 | 19 | 10 | 6 | 1 | **2.80** |

35 lands, four land-fetchers (Nature's Lore, Farseek, Three Visits, Cultivate), Sakura-Tribe Elder,
two dorks (Birds, Llanowar Elves), Sol Ring, and two extra land drops (Oracle of Mul Daya, Icetill
Explorer). X spells (March of the Multitudes, Pest Infestation) count as MV 1–3 here but scale
with whatever mana you have. **Ghave on turn 4 is the normal case.**

## 2. How Ghave's two abilities work together

- **{1}, remove a +1/+1 counter from *any* creature you control → Saproling.** Not just Ghave. A
  Tireless Tracker's counters (one per Clue you crack), Avenger's Plants (a counter on **each** Plant
  per land) and a board pumped by Gavony Township are all in the bank too.
- **{1}, sacrifice a creature → +1/+1 counter on target creature.** It's an instant-speed outlet
  that also refills the bank. Target Ghave to keep him alive, or a Saproling to feed Skullclamp
  later.
- **Never remove his last counter by accident.** He is a 0/0 and dies immediately (CR 704.5f).
- Hardened Scales / Kami of Whispered Hopes make each sacrifice worth **2 counters** (3 with both).
  Doubling Season makes it 2, and doubles the Saprolings as well.

## 3. What makes tokens without anything dying first

**Cheap:** Bitterblossom, Springheart Nantuko, Brightcap Badger (its Fungus Frolic half), Adeline,
Lingering Souls, Scute Swarm, Slimefoot, Rosie Cotton, Black Market Connections, Pest Infestation.
**Burst / bigger:** March of the Multitudes (X, instant, convoke), Second Harvest, Tendershoot Dryad,
Avenger of Zendikar, Elspeth, Storm Slayer.
That's 15 cards plus the commander. By turn 3 on the draw you see one of the cheap ones 67% of the
time, and **Ghave covers the rest.**

**Landfall (added 2026-09-25).** Every land you play is a trigger. Tireless Tracker makes a Clue (card
draw), Springheart Nantuko makes a 1/1, Scute Swarm copies itself once you have six lands, and Avenger's
Plants each get a counter. Doublers double the Clues and Insects, and Chatterfang adds a Squirrel to each.
Oracle of Mul Daya gives you a second land drop and lets you play lands off the top of your library.
Icetill Explorer gives another drop and lets you play lands **from your graveyard**. Fetchlands
(Marsh Flats, Verdant Catacombs, Windswept Heath) are **two** triggers each, and with Icetill you can
replay the same fetch every turn. Icetill mills a card per landfall, which can bin more fetches to replay.

**Springheart Nantuko as a copier:** bestow it ({1}{G}) on a nonlegendary token engine (Tendershoot
Dryad, Brightcap Badger, Scute Swarm, Tireless Tracker). Then every land lets you pay {1}{G} for a
token copy of that engine. Don't bestow it on Rosie, Slimefoot, Adeline or Chatterfang, because a
copy of a legend dies to the legend rule.

## 4. Opening

**T1–T2:** a dork (Birds, Llanowar Elves), a land-fetcher, or Hardened Scales. Then Bitterblossom,
Springheart Nantuko, Viscera Seer or Skullclamp.

**T3:** Adeline, Tireless Tracker, Lingering Souls, Rosie Cotton, Slimefoot, Scute Swarm, Kami of
Whispered Hopes, or an Altar. Cast landfall creatures **before** your land drop.

**T4: Ghave.** Leave {1}–{2} open after casting him if you can. Those are two instant-speed
Saprolings, or an answer to removal (sacrifice in response).

**T5–T6:** a doubler (Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, Mondrak, Doubling Season, Elspeth), then
Grave Pact / Dictate / drainers.

**Mulligans:** keep 2–3 lands and a ramp piece. You don't need Ghave in the opener, since he's in
the command zone.

## 5. The engines to look for

- **Ghave + a free outlet + Blood Artist / Zulaport.** Each {1} is a body; each sacrifice is a
  drain *and* the counter back.
- **Slimefoot:** *"Whenever a Saproling you control dies, Slimefoot deals 1 damage to each opponent
  and you gain 1 life."* Every Ghave Saproling is a Zulaport on its own. Slimefoot's own {4}
  makes Saprolings too.
- **Skullclamp on a Saproling is two cards for {1}.** Ghave refills the Saproling for {1}.
- **Moldervine Reclamation:** a card and a life for every creature death. Your draw engine once the
  board is going.
- **Mentor of the Meek:** pay {1} per Saproling to draw. It's your mana sink when there's nothing
  better.
- **Grave Pact / Dictate of Erebos:** every Saproling you sacrifice makes each opponent sacrifice a
  creature. Two Saprolings clear two blockers from every opponent.

## 6. Sequencing within your turn

1. **Upkeep:** Bitterblossom, Tendershoot (every upkeep, **including opponents'**). With Oracle out,
   check the top of your library before you draw.
2. **Main 1:** landfall payoffs first, then your land drop(s). Doublers and engines go down *before*
   you spend counters, so every Saproling is doubled.
3. **Attack:** Adeline makes one Human per opponent, already attacking. Gavony Township makes the
   whole board bigger. Attacking tokens that get blocked still
   drain on the way out.
4. **End step / opponents' turns:** Ghave works at instant speed. Hold mana and make Saprolings at the
   end of the turn before yours, or in response to a wipe (sacrifice everything for drain and
   Grave Pact edicts before it resolves).

## 7. The kill

- **Grind:** every turn, Saprolings in, sacrifices out, drain and Grave Pact edicts. Adeline and
  Gavony Township keep the combat route live. Wide boards with counters are hard to block.
- **Burst:** March of the Multitudes at the end of the turn before yours. Convoke with your tokens and
  untap into a doubled board. Or Avenger after a big land turn, Second Harvest before you sacrifice
  everything, or Meathook with drainers out.
- **Combo (declared):** see §8.

## 8. The declared combos (chosen-N; announce each iteration)

Every line is **Ghave + two cards**. Opponents can respond between iterations.

| Pieces | Per iteration |
|---|---|
| **Ashnod's Altar + any token doubler** | Remove a counter ({1}) → 2 Saprolings. Altar one ({2}). Sacrifice the other to Ghave ({1}) → counter back. **0 mana, 2 deaths.** |
| **Phyrexian Altar + Doubling Season** | Two removals → 4 Saprolings. 3 into the Altar, 1 into Ghave → 2 counters back. **0 mana, 4 deaths.** |
| **Phyrexian Altar + two token doublers** | 4 Saprolings per removal. 3 into the Altar, 1 into Ghave. **+{1}, 4 deaths.** |
| **Pitiless Plunderer + any token doubler** | 2 Saprolings. Sacrifice one to Ghave → 2 Treasures pay both activations. **+1 Saproling, 1 death.** |
| **Rosie Cotton + Ashnod's Altar** (or Phyrexian) | Remove Ghave's counter ({1}) → Saproling → Rosie puts a counter back on Ghave. Altar the Saproling. **+{1} with Ashnod's (infinite mana), 0 with Phyrexian.** |

**Chatterfang counts as a token doubler in every line above** (a Squirrel for every Saproling). It
stacks with the other doublers in either order — doublers commute (LEDGER 2026-09-24).

**The one line that skips Ghave — Chatterfang + Pitiless Plunderer** (added 2026-09-24, pilot opted
in to combos): sacrifice a Squirrel to Chatterfang's `{B}` → Plunderer makes a Treasure → Chatterfang
adds a Squirrel → the Treasure pays the next `{B}`. One death and −1 toughness on a target per
iteration, nothing spent. With no opposing creature, target the Squirrel you are sacrificing
(CR 601.2c/601.2h, 608.2b). Tell the table it's in the deck.

**Not a loop:** one token-only doubler (Procession, Parallel Lives, Mondrak, Elspeth) with
Phyrexian Altar. That's −{1} per iteration.

**Any drainer ends it.** Slimefoot alone is 1 damage to each opponent per Saproling.

## 9. Mistakes to avoid

1. **Removing Ghave's last counter** with no sacrifice ready to put one back. He dies as a 0/0.
2. **Spending counters before the doubler lands.** Cast Procession / Parallel Lives / Doubling Season
   first.
3. **Ordering the counter effects wrong.** Apply Hardened Scales / Kami (+1) **before** Doubling
   Season (×2). You choose the order (CR 616.1e).
4. **Tapping Saprolings made this turn** for Cryptolith Rite or Brightcap Badger: they're summoning
   sick. They *can* still be sacrificed.
5. **Clamping a creature with a +1/+1 counter.** It survives. Remove the counter with Ghave first.
6. **Meathook with X ≥ 1** kills your dorks and 1/1s. Only do it when the deaths are the point.
7. **Mentor of the Meek during an infinite loop:** draw only what you need. Decking yourself is a
   loss.
