# Teysa Karlov — Pilot's Gameplan

## 0. The one-paragraph version

**Every death happens twice.** You are not trying to build the widest board in the game — you are
trying to make each individual death worth as much as possible, then have a lot of them. A 1/1
Spirit dying with Teysa and three drainers out is worth six triggers. Your creatures also leave
something behind when they die, so removal and wraths are less punishing for you than for anyone
else at the table.

## 1. The curve you actually have

| | MV≤1 | MV2 | MV3 | MV4 | MV5 | MV6+ | avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nonland (64) | 11 | 20 | 19 | 11 | 3 | 0 | **2.61** |

MV≤2 is 31 cards and **nothing costs more than 5**. You should never be stuck.

## 2. What Teysa does and does not double

**DOES double** (the event is a creature dying): Blood Artist · Zulaport Cutthroat · Cruel Celebrant ·
Bastion of Remembrance · Elas il-Kor · Falkenrath Noble · Marionette Apprentice · every afterlife
trigger · Open the Graves · Elenda's death tokens · Hallowed Spiritkeeper · Pitiless Plunderer ·
Midnight Reaper · Grim Haruspex · **Skullclamp (four cards)**.

**Does NOT double:** Mirkwood Bats (triggers on *creating* and *sacrificing* a token, not on dying) ·
Marauding Blight-Priest and Vito (life-gain triggers) · Oketra's Monument and Bontu's Monument (cast
triggers) · Thalisse (end-step trigger) · Hidden Stockpile (end-step trigger).

**She still doubles everything when she dies in the same wipe** — dies-triggers look back in time.

## 3. The three numbers to track

1. **Deaths this turn** — and remember each is two of every doubled trigger.
2. **Life-gain EVENTS, not life** — Blight-Priest and Vito count sources. Teysa doubling Blood Artist
   means *two* gain events per death, so Blight-Priest fires twice too.
3. **Is Bloodletter out on your turn?** Everything doubles again.

## 4. Opening

**T1–T2:** Doomed Traveler, Hunted Witness, Viscera Seer, Carrion Feeder, Bitterblossom, Skullclamp,
Orzhov Enforcer, Imperious Oligarch, Hidden Stockpile. Every one of these is two bodies with Teysa.

**T3 — the fork:**
- Board already → **Teysa**. She is a multiplier; cast her when there is something to multiply.
- No board → **Oketra's Monument** (a token for every creature spell from here on) or **Ophiomancer**.
- **Bontu's Monument** if the hand is creature-heavy — it discounts them *and* drains on every cast.

**T4–T6:** Anointed Procession or Mondrak, then a drainer, then start sacrificing.

**Mulligans.** Keep 3 lands with both colours and a 1–2 drop. You do not need Teysa in the opener.

## 5. The engine to look for

**Free outlet + Blood Artist + Zulaport + Teysa.** One 1/1 Spirit sacrificed is 2 focused (Artist ×2)
and 2 to each opponent (Zulaport ×2), plus 4 life to you, which is 4 Blight-Priest triggers — **another
4 to each.** That is roughly 20 across the table for one token, before Bastion or Cruel Celebrant.

**Skullclamp on any 1/1 is four cards.** Equip, it dies, draw four. This is the best draw engine in
either deck and it is why Divine Visitation and Cathars' Crusade are deliberately absent.

## 6. Sequencing within your turn

1. **Upkeep** — Bitterblossom, Ophiomancer, Phyrexian Arena.
2. **Main 1 — Bloodletter first if you have it.** Then bodies. Bontu's Monument drains as you cast.
3. **Attack with tokens.** Teysa gives them **vigilance and lifelink**, so attacking costs you no
   blockers and every attacking token is a separate life-gain event for Blight-Priest and Vito.
   Vault of the Archangel adds deathtouch on top.
4. **Sacrifice in the second main**, after combat damage and after Thalisse would count.
5. **End step** — Thalisse creates X Spirits where X is tokens made this turn, and Hidden Stockpile
   adds a Servo. **Do your sacrificing before Thalisse's trigger only if the extra deaths are lethal;
   otherwise let her count first and sacrifice on the next turn.**

## 7. The kill

Two routes:

- **Grind:** attack with lifelinking tokens each turn with Blight-Priest and Vito out. Ten attacking
  tokens is ten gain events — ten to each opponent from Blight-Priest and ten more from Vito to one.
- **Burst:** Kaya's Wrath or The Meathook Massacre with three drainers out. Teysa doubles every death
  trigger, and dies-triggers look back, so your own drainers dying in the wipe still pay. **Check
  Blight-Priest and Vito are expendable first — they have no look-back and contribute nothing.**

## 8. The declared combo (assembles ~turn 6–7)

**Teysa + Pitiless Plunderer + Reassembling Skeleton + a free outlet.**

Sacrifice the Skeleton → Teysa doubles Plunderer → **two Treasures** → pay {1}{B} to return the
Skeleton → repeat. Net zero mana. Each loop is two Blood Artist triggers, two Zulaport, two Bastion.
Announce every iteration and let opponents respond; stop when the table is dead.

Cutting **Reassembling Skeleton** removes this entirely. Ashnod's Altar alone does not enable it
({C}{C} cannot pay the Skeleton's {B}).

## 9. Mistakes to avoid

1. **Casting Teysa on an empty board** into open mana. She multiplies; she does not create.
2. **Forgetting she does NOT double Mirkwood Bats, Blight-Priest or Vito.**
3. **Forgetting tokens have lifelink and vigilance** — attack; it is free and each attacker is a
   gain event.
4. **Wiping while Blight-Priest or Vito are your only payoffs.**
5. **Equipping Skullclamp to something with toughness 2** — it needs to be a 1/1 to die.
6. **Sacrificing before your end step when Thalisse is out** and the extra Spirits would have been
   bigger than the drain.
7. **Forgetting Open the Graves, Midnight Reaper and Grim Haruspex are NONTOKEN-only.** Your Spirit
   tokens dying do not trigger them.
