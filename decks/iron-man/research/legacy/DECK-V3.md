# Iron Man — Izzet Artifact Voltron (V3: engine + insurance)

Commander: Tony Stark // The Invincible Iron Man (UR)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Ancient Tomb · Fierce Guardianship · The One Ring

> **Experimental V3, built 2026-08-21 to test one thesis against V2:** the deck's losses
> (can't finish, gets 2-for-1'd on Equipment, no repeatable draw) are all one problem — not
> enough card flow to reach or rebuild an already-lethal finisher. V3 trims the additive
> Equipment tier and spends those slots on an **artifacts-entering draw engine** (direction A)
> plus **anti-targeted-removal insurance** (direction B).
>
> `DECK.md` is untouched V1; `DECK-V2.md` is preserved for A/B comparison. Grounds:
> `research/v3-engine-insurance-2026-08-21.md`.
