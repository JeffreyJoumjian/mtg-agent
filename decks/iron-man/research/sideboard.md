# Iron Man — sideboard

Single source of truth for cards held outside the 100. Every entry names what it **displaces**.

The deck is at **exactly 100** with **3/3 Game Changers**, so any Game Changer you promote is a
fourth and takes the deck to bracket 4.

**Already at 3.5:** the maindeck keeps **Aggravated Assault**, which goes infinite with *two*
Equipment the commander attaches for free — The Reaver Cleaver (damage → Treasures → pay
`{3}{R}{R}`) and Sword of Feast and Famine (damage → untap all lands → pay again). WotC permits
bracket 3 decks where *"the long game could end with one being deployed"*, and with 7 extra-combat
effects the card is core to the design — but it's redundantly assembled. **Cut Aggravated Assault
for Master Transmuter** if a pod wants a strict bracket 3.

## Evaluated and passed — with the grounds, so they can be re-checked

- **Liquimetal Torque** — 1/5 field, taps only for `{C}`, and *"target nonland permanent becomes an
  artifact"* is near-dead with no maindeck artifact removal. Bring it back alongside Vandalblast if
  you want the "turn their bomb into an artifact, then blast it" line.
- **Sword of Fire and Ice** — kept on the user's call twice (2026-08-07), then **cut for Hammer of
  Nazahn on 2026-08-19, also the user's call**, once Wizard's Staff grew its CR 702.16d conflict
  list to five maindeck Equipment. Now a situational row above.
- **Kaldra Compleat / Nettlecyst** — Living weapon (CR 702.92a) attaches them to their own Germ
  token, and all their bonuses sit on the equipped creature. Fine as standalone threats, useless as
  voltron payload. **Note: Hexplate Wallbreaker is NOT in this category** and is maindecked — its
  untap and extra-combat clauses are global, so it works from a token.
- **Lightning Greaves / Whispersilk Cloak** — grant **shroud**; you could no longer equip or target
  your own commander. The free combat attach still works (CR 701.3a — attaching doesn't target),
  but nothing else does.
- **Gauntlet of Power** — symmetric on both clauses (*"creatures of the chosen color"*, *"its
  controller adds"*) and the mana half only reads **basic** lands, of which the deck runs 10.
- **Iron Man, Tony Stark** — **now maindecked.** Passed initially, reversed once Roaming Throne
  made it a third doubled Hero trigger. See `research/decisions.md`.
- **Hulking Metamorph** — 1/5 field, `{9}` or prototype `{2}{U}{U}` for a copy of something you
  already control. Cut for Iron Man, Tony Stark.
- **Vision of Love** (`{1}{R}`, $0.21) — instant, sacrifice an artifact or discard to draw two.
  Genuinely good with 6 Treasure makers, and the sacrifice feeds Goblin Welder / Goblin Engineer /
  Academy Ruins. Lost to **Insight Engine**, whose charge counters accumulate — `{2}` for 1 card,
  then 2, then 3 — so it out-draws a one-shot from the third activation on, and it's an artifact.
  It used to point at **Big Score**, which left the 100 on 2026-08-19; Big Score is now the
  cheaper way to buy that slot back, so bring Vision of Love in only if you specifically want
  the artifact sacrifice. Either one displaces **Conqueror's Flail**.
- **Mana Geyser** — a sorcery, so opponents' lands are mostly untapped on your main phase; and the
  commander already deploys your expensive cards free.
- **Machine God's Effigy** — the obvious line (copy the commander) is blanked by the legend rule.
- **Panharmonicon** — does **not** double the commander's trigger; it only doubles triggers caused
  by something *entering*. Roaming Throne is the card that does the job.
- **Worldwalker Helm** — needs a token engine, not loose token sources.
- **One with the Machine** — cut 2026-08-19 for Fateful Discovery. One-shot draw keyed to the
  biggest-MV artifact already on board; the engine draws off every artifact ETB instead and
  survives all three of our own sweepers. Re-check if the deck ever drops its Treasure density.
- **Cursed Mirror** — cut 2026-08-19 for Wizard's Staff. The marginal 8th rock (field avg is 7),
  2/5 signal; the until-EOT copy mode was a bonus, not a role.
- **Training Grounds** ($10) — passed for now. Its only live text here is the commander's
  transform: `{4}{U}{R}` → `{2}{U}{R}` (can't reduce below one mana; the front-face loot is
  already `{1}`, Welder is free). Revisit if the flip turn still feels slow after Command Beacon.
- **Iron Man, Armored Avenger** ($4) — closest miss of the eight name-cards not run. Every draw
  puts a +1/+1 counter on any creature, and Roaming Throne (Hero) doubles it — but it's a
  structural creature add (10 is load-bearing) and a 2/2 removal magnet.
- **Long-Lost Lances** — team-wide first strike/vigilance wants many equipped bodies; this deck
  has one carrier, and all 17 incumbents do more for him.
- **Venser's Journal / Spellbook** — no-max-hand-size is covered by Thought Vessel and mooted by
  The Ten Rings' refill-to-ten; the deck empties its hand through free deploys anyway.
- **The Arkenstone** — **illegal**: the artifact face is colourless but its Adventure half is
  `{2}{W}`, so colour identity is White (see LEDGER 2026-08-19).
- **Brass Squire / Magnetic Theft** — the rest of the Izzet-legal attach pool. Squire is a
  repeatable battlefield re-attach but a structural creature slot (only the budget list runs it);
  Theft is a one-shot at 0/5 field. Thorin (above, HOB review) covers the re-suit case better.
- **The "power-damage" family** (swept 2026-08-19, 77 + 12 cards, all 0/5 field) — the deck
  already owns the best one: **Chandra's Ignition**, which Mjölnir doubles. Everything else lost
  to the extra combats it would displace: an extra combat is *commander damage* + another ×3
  deploy trigger + attack triggers, where a fling is raw damage only and dead without a board.
  Soul's Fire (row above) is the one kept for combat-denial pods. Near-misses: **Fiendlash**
  ({1}{R} — an indestructible commander + your own Blasphemous Act flings 2× power with Mjölnir;
  fun, but needs three pieces timed together) · **Nibelheim Aflame** (Ignition minus the face
  damage) · **Fall of the Hammer / Tail Slash / HULK SMASH!** (removal role is already double the
  field) · **Surestrike Trident / Burning Anger** (tap-to-fire fights the attack step) ·
  **Warstorm Surge / Electropotence** (keyed to *creature* ETBs in a deck that enters artifacts) ·
  **Gravitic Punch** (sorcery Soul's Fire) · **Fling / Thud / Kazuul's Fury** (sacrificing the
  commander to kill one of three opponents is bad multiplayer math).
- **The goad / mass-tap family** (swept 2026-08-19, all 0/5 field) — mostly solving a problem the
  deck pre-solved: the commander **flies** (only fliers/reach can block), tramples through chumps,
  Ultima Weapon shoots a blocker before blocks, Conqueror's Flail stops flash blockers, and
  Rogue's Passage sits in the sideboard. Disrupt Decorum (row above) is the one worth holding.
  Passed: **Icy Blast** (ferocious is always on at commander power 5+, but X mana competes with
  counter/recast mana) · **Sleep** (one player only) · **Bident of Thassa** ("attack this turn if
  able" has no "other than you" clause — it can point the attack at *us*) · **Besmirch** (one
  creature) · **Goblin Spymaster**-style symmetric forced attacks (no "not you" clause either).
- **Assassin Gauntlet** (`{2}{U}`, 2026-09-03) — the ETB works as hoped off the combat trigger:
  choose zero for the attach target (CR 115.6), tap one opponent's team, they can't block
  (CR 509.1a). But it is one opponent, once; Captain America's Shield taps a blocker every attack
  and the commander flies. +1/+1 is the additive tier V3 trimmed, and the loot is selection, not
  draw. Re-check if games are being lost to one opponent's wall of fliers.
- **Chainsaw** (`{1}{R}`, 2026-09-03) — *"whenever one or more creatures die"* is one rev counter
  per **event** (CR 603.2c), and this deck makes almost no death events of its own (Ignition,
  Mjölnir's ETB, its own ETB — one each). Expect +2 to +4 by the time it matters. The ETB 3 damage
  is the real content and Mjölnir already does 4. Equip `{3}`.
- **Infantry Shield** (`{2}{R}`, 2026-09-02, re-checked 2026-09-03 as a Chainsaw "combo") — the
  mobilize tokens are red Warriors, not artifacts, never block (sacrificed at your end step,
  CR 702.181a), and die as **one** event, so the combo is one Chainsaw counter per attack, not X.
  Their damage is life-total damage split across opponents (CR 508.4), not commander damage. It
  went to **Edgar** instead, where each token is a per-creature enters- and dies-trigger.
