# Captain America, Living Legend — Tap Engine (rebuild)

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Cyclonic Rift · Fierce Guardianship · Teferi's Protection

> Built from scratch 2026-09-10 to answer the pilot's question: *what is the most reliably consistent
> way to maximise his ability?* **Every creature gets two meaningful taps per turn.** The first tap
> crews a Vehicle or stations a Spacecraft (or feeds Grand Architect, Relic of Legends, convoke);
> Cap untaps it; the second tap uses the creature's own ability — draw, mana, removal, protection.
> "Becomes tapped" payoffs (Fallowsage, Tui and La, Mechan Navigator, Sanwell, Unctus's loot on every
> blue creature) fire on BOTH taps, because crewing taps them.
>
> **Resilience pass 2026-09-16**, after play: a turn 4–5 wipe took the whole engine and left nothing to
> crew the Vehicles. The list now holds 10 answers to a wipe — mostly effects that let the wipe resolve
> and keep your board (phasing, Eerie Interlude, indestructible, Faith's Reward) — and 8 sources of crew
> that survive a creature wipe (The Wandering Emperor, Gideon, Hangarback Walker, Mishra's Factory,
> Castle Ardenvale, Shorikai, Urza's Saga, Mech Hangar). Command Beacon takes the tax off a recast Cap.
>
> The Vehicles and Spacecraft are the win condition: many independent threats, crewed for free, and the
> kill doesn't depend on Cap surviving. Creatures you double-tap stay tapped through opponents' turns,
> where Adept Watershaper (indestructible), The Seriema (legends) and The Wandering Rescuer (hexproof)
> protect them.
>
> **No infinite loops (mtg-rules-expert: loop audit 2026-09-10, blink audit 2026-09-15).** Every payoff
> needs a tap and every untap pays for exactly one tap. The only repeatable cycles are Ioreth ⇄
> Thousand-Year Elixir ({1} a cycle) and Ioreth ⇄ Minamo ({U} a cycle) — finite mana sinks. A blinked
> creature is "new" for Cap's first-tap check, so each blink buys one extra untap; the blink here is
> one-shot (Eerie Interlude, Restoration Angel). **Do not add:** Deadeye Navigator (with Relic of
> Legends alone it gives unlimited blinks; with Elixir plus mana creatures, unlimited mana); Marvin,
> Murderous Mimic; a second "untap target permanent" creature (Kelpie Guide, Vizier of Tumbling Sands,
> Aphetto Alchemist); a legendary creature with "{T}: untap"; an untapper that doesn't tap itself
> (Retrofitter Foundry); or anything that adds mana when a permanent taps or untaps. Re-audit before
> adding any other repeatable instant-speed blink.
>
> Sits beside DECK.md (Vehicles) and DECK-COUNTERS.md (lifelink voltron). Grounds: research/decisions.md.
