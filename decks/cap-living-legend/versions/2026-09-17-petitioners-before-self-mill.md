# Captain America, Living Legend — Pure Petitioners Mill

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Grand Arbiter Augustin IV · Rhystic Study · Teferi's Protection

> Built 2026-09-16 to the pilot's brief: *"purely mill using petitioners as many of them as possible while
> keeping the cards that make opponents mill more than they should."* No self-mill, no Thassa's Oracle, no
> Vehicles — mill the table out and nothing else.
>
> **33 Persistent Petitioners.** Legal because the card says so: *"A deck can have any number of cards named
> Persistent Petitioners."*
>
> **The rate.** "Tap four untapped Advisors you control: Target player mills twelve cards." Cap untaps each
> Advisor's FIRST tap, so the same four Advisors activate twice — the deck mills **6 cards per Advisor per
> turn**, doubled to 12 by Bruvac. On a board of eight Advisors that is 48 a turn, or 96 with Bruvac out.
>
> | Advisors on board | Your turn | + Bruvac | Per rotation w/ Drumbellower | + Bruvac |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | 4 | 24 | 48 | 60 | 120 |
> | 8 | 48 | 96 | 120 | 240 |
> | 12 | 72 | 144 | 180 | 360 |
>
> **"Target player" is singular** — a three-opponent pod is ~297 cards and three separate jobs. Spread the
> activations or focus one player out at a time; do not expect a simultaneous table kill.
>
> **Drumbellower is the multiplier.** "Untap all creatures you control during each other player's untap
> step." The mill ability has no timing restriction, so you activate it on their turns too: each Advisor goes
> from 2 taps a turn to **5 per rotation** (2 on yours, 1 on each opponent's). That is 2.5x the rate, and the
> loop audit cleared it — untap steps are turn-based actions, once per turn.
>
> **Cost reducers, not more mana.** Herald's Horn and Urza's Incubator naming **Advisor** make every
> Petitioner cost {U} (Incubator drops Bruvac to {U} as well), and Grand Arbiter Augustin IV does it while
> being an Advisor himself. This is the trade that mana creatures failed: a reducer makes all 33 Petitioners
> cheaper instead of adding a body that can't join the tap-four.
>
> **The three amplifiers** are everything in W/U that makes opponents mill more than the card says:
> Bruvac the Grandiloquent (they mill double — and he is himself a Human **Advisor**, so he feeds the
> engine), The Water Crystal (+4 per mill, plus a tap ability that mills each opponent for your hand size),
> and Fraying Sanity (enchanted player mills again at EACH end step, counting every card that hit their
> graveyard from anywhere). With Bruvac and The Water Crystal both out, **the milled opponent chooses which
> replacement applies first** (CR 616.1) and will pick the cheaper one — plan on the smaller number.
>
> **Masako the Humorless is load-bearing**, not filler: "tapped creatures you control can block as though
> they were untapped." This deck taps its whole board every turn and would otherwise never block. She is
> also an Advisor. Adept Watershaper makes those tapped Petitioners indestructible.
>
> **Raise the Past is the reset button.** Every Petitioner is mana value 2, so it returns the entire
> graveyard's worth of them at once after a sweeper. Return to the Ranks does it at instant-speed cost with
> convoke — and your Petitioners can convoke it.
>
> **Mana from tapping creatures (added 2026-09-16).** No card in W/U reads "whenever a creature becomes
> tapped, add mana" — that effect only exists as mana creatures and as lands that tap a creature for mana.
> This list takes only the cost-tap version: Holdout Settlement and Survivors' Encampment ("{T}, Tap an
> untapped creature you control: Add one mana of any color") plus Springleaf Drum. Tap a Petitioner for mana,
> Cap untaps it, and it is still available for the tap-four-Advisors activation. Two of the three are land
> slots, so they cost nothing to include — **but the mana is only free when you have spare taps.** With N
> Advisors on board you have 2N taps and floor(N/2) activations: an EVEN count leaves zero taps over, an ODD
> count leaves exactly two. Check the parity before spending a tap on mana.
>
> **Mana creatures were tried and reverted (2026-09-16).** Palladium Myr, Silver Myr, Gold Myr and Hedron
> Crawler went in and came straight back out. The headline ability costs **zero mana**, so mana was never the
> bottleneck — Advisors on the battlefield are. A myr is a body that does not count toward the four, so four
> of them cost 24 mill a turn (48 under Bruvac) to buy mana the engine never spends. Omen Hawker remains the
> only card in the colours that is both a mana creature and an Advisor.
>
> **Deliberately absent:** Mesmeric Orb (your own untap step would mill you faster than it mills them, and
> this list has no Laboratory Maniac insurance), Thassa's Oracle and all self-mill, Kelpie Guide / Marvin /
> Aphetto Alchemist / Basalt Monolith (infinite untap loops — see DECK-MILL.md's header), Painter's
> Servant and Rest in Peace (deterministic two-card library-emptying combos, both legal in W/U).
>
> **Honest note on the count:** past roughly 20 Petitioners the extra copies buy redundancy rather than
> speed — you only ever need four Advisors on the battlefield to switch the engine on. 33 is what "as many as possible"
> looks like once the deck can actually cast them; 20-24 with more interaction would win more games. Easy to dial back.
>
> **Renamed 2026-09-17** — this list was `DECK-MILL.md` until then; `research/decisions.md` entries and
> `versions/*mill-before*` snapshots from before that date use the old name.
>
