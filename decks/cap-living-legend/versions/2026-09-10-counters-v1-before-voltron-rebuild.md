# Captain America, Living Legend — Azorius Counters Voltron (variant)

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Cyclonic Rift · Enlightened Tutor · Teferi's Protection

> Variant of `DECK.md`, built 2026-09-10. Same engine — free first untap each turn, draw engines,
> opponent-turn untappers — different win condition. **Cap himself is the threat:** every card drawn
> becomes a +1/+1 counter on him (Iron Man, Lyla, Stark's Ingenuity, Wizard Class), his own untap
> doubles the tap-based counter engines (Agent Phil Coulson hits Heroes, Keensight Mentor hits
> vigilance — Cap is both), and K-9 / The Destined Thief / Rogue's Passage make him unblockable.
> Modelled clock: lethal 21 commander damage on the second swing once Iron Man + Coulson are out.
>
> No Vehicles, no Station. 13 of the main list's 17 finishers are artifacts; this list's win
> condition is a creature plus enchantments, so it survives the artifact wipe that ends `DECK.md`.
> Grounds: `research/counters-variant-2026-09-10.md`. `STATUS.md` mirrors `DECK.md` only.
