# Captain America, Living Legend — Tap → Tokens → Drain

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Cyclonic Rift · Fierce Guardianship · Teferi's Protection

> **How it wins.** Throne of the God-Pharaoh drains *each* opponent for the number of tapped creatures you
> control at your end step. Psychosis Crawler drains each opponent for every card you draw. Halo Fountain
> wins outright once you can untap fifteen tapped creatures. None of it needs combat, evasion or an
> unblocked attacker — the 2026-09-15 play report killed the big-Vehicles plan: a 20/20 attacks one
> opponent and gets chump-blocked by a 1/1, and the pod has 120 life between them.
>
> **The engine.** Cap untaps the FIRST tap of each creature each turn, so every creature gets two tap
> events a turn. Tokens are the fuel: makers feed the doublers, and every tapped body feeds Throne.
>
> **The free double-tap pass** — the core play (verified 2026-09-16):
> 1. Crew a Vehicle, tapping every creature. Cap untaps them all.
> 2. Crew the same Vehicle again — no Cap trigger this time, so they all stay tapped (crew has no
>    frequency limit, CR 702.122a).
> 3. Opposition targets a creature and pays its cost by tapping that same creature: targets are locked at
>    601.2c before costs are paid at 601.2h, so Cap's untap resolves first and the ability then re-taps it.
>    One activation, no mana, one creature left tapped.
>
> Do this in RESPONSE to Throne's end-step trigger so blockers stay up all turn; with Myrel out, opponents
> can't interact during your turn at all. Adeline's tokens ENTER tapped, which is not "becomes tapped"
> (603.2e), so Cap never untaps them and they count for Throne for free.
>
> **Watch your own library.** Unctus grants every other blue creature a *mandatory* loot when it becomes
> tapped, and the Faerie and Merfolk tokens are blue — two forced draws per blue creature per turn. Count
> before a big tapping pass (CR 104.3c).
>
> **Resilience (2026-09-16).** 10 answers to a board wipe, mostly ones that let it resolve while your board
> survives, plus 8 sources of crew that survive a creature wipe. Command Beacon takes the tax off a recast Cap.
>
> **No unbounded loops** (mtg-rules-expert: 2026-09-10, blink 2026-09-15, token/drain 2026-09-16). Halo
> Fountain ⇄ Ioreth is a mana sink, not a loop: {W} per token, {W}{W} per card. **Never add:**
> 1. **A creature that taps for two or more mana that can pay {W}** (Vedalken Engineer) — with Halo Fountain
>    + Ioreth that is free, unbounded card draw, and Psychosis Crawler turns it into a table kill.
> 2. **A second "{T}: untap target permanent" creature** — Marvin, Murderous Mimic; Kelpie Guide; Vizier of
>    Tumbling Sands; Aphetto Alchemist; Deadeye Navigator. Two of them untap each other free, forever.
> 3. **Intruder Alarm**, or anything untapping creatures when a creature enters — with Opposition plus a
>    token-on-tap creature it is free and unbounded. The most dangerous single card for this build.
> 4. **Anything adding mana when a permanent becomes tapped or untapped**, or untapping Halo Fountain
>    cheaply (Clock of Omens). Unwinding Clock and Seedborn Muse are untap-step multipliers — re-audit first.
> 5. **An extra-combat enabler alongside Myrel** — her attack trigger is the deck's only exponential.
>
> Sits beside DECK.md (Vehicles) and DECK-COUNTERS.md (lifelink voltron). Grounds: research/decisions.md.
