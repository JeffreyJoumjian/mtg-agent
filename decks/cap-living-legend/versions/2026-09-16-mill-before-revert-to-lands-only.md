# Captain America, Living Legend — Pure Petitioners Mill

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (2/3): Rhystic Study · Teferi's Protection

> Built 2026-09-16 to the pilot's brief: *"purely mill using petitioners as many of them as possible while
> keeping the cards that make opponents mill more than they should."* No self-mill, no Thassa's Oracle, no
> Vehicles — mill the table out and nothing else.
>
> **31 Persistent Petitioners.** Legal because the card says so: *"A deck can have any number of cards named
> Persistent Petitioners."*
>
> **The rate.** "Tap four untapped Advisors you control: Target player mills twelve cards." Cap untaps each
> Advisor's FIRST tap, so the same four Advisors activate twice — the deck mills **6 cards per Advisor per
> turn**, doubled to 12 by Bruvac. On a board of eight Advisors that is 48 a turn, or 96 with Bruvac out.
>
> | Advisors on board | Mill per turn | With Bruvac |
> |---|---|---|
> | 4 | 24 | 48 |
> | 8 | 48 | 96 |
> | 12 | 72 | 144 |
>
> **"Target player" is singular** — a three-opponent pod is ~297 cards and three separate jobs. Spread the
> activations or focus one player out at a time; do not expect a simultaneous table kill.
>
> **The three amplifiers** are everything in W/U that makes opponents mill more than the card says:
> Bruvac the Grandiloquent (they mill double — and he is himself a Human **Advisor**, so he feeds the
> engine), The Water Crystal (+4 per mill, plus a tap ability that mills each opponent for your hand size),
> and Fraying Sanity (enchanted player mills again at EACH end step, counting every card that hit their
> graveyard from anywhere). With Bruvac and The Water Crystal both out, **the milled opponent chooses which
> replacement applies first** (CR 616.1) and will pick the cheaper one — plan on the smaller number.
>
> **Masako the Humorless is load-bearing**, not filler: "tapped creatures you control can block as though
> they were untapped." This deck taps its whole board every turn and would otherwise never block. She is
> also an Advisor. Adept Watershaper makes those tapped Petitioners indestructible.
>
> **Raise the Past is the reset button.** Every Petitioner is mana value 2, so it returns the entire
> graveyard's worth of them at once after a sweeper. Return to the Ranks does it at instant-speed cost with
> convoke — and your Petitioners can convoke it.
>
> **Mana from tapping creatures (added 2026-09-16).** No card in W/U reads "whenever a creature becomes
> tapped, add mana" — that effect only exists as mana creatures and as lands that tap a creature for mana.
> Holdout Settlement and Survivors' Encampment ("{T}, Tap an untapped creature you control: Add one mana of
> any color") plus Springleaf Drum turn a Petitioner's free Cap untap into mana: tap it for mana, Cap untaps
> it, and it is still available for the tap-four-Advisors activation. Palladium Myr, Silver Myr, Gold Myr and
> Hedron Crawler each tap twice a turn under Cap. Note none of them is an **Advisor**, so they do not feed
> the mill-12 activation — that is the cost of the four Petitioner slots they replaced. Omen Hawker remains
> the only card that is both a mana creature and an Advisor.
>
> **Deliberately absent:** Mesmeric Orb (your own untap step would mill you faster than it mills them, and
> this list has no Laboratory Maniac insurance), Thassa's Oracle and all self-mill, Kelpie Guide / Marvin /
> Aphetto Alchemist / Basalt Monolith (infinite untap loops — see DECK-PETITIONERS.md's header), Painter's
> Servant and Rest in Peace (deterministic two-card library-emptying combos, both legal in W/U).
>
> **Honest note on the count:** past roughly 20 Petitioners the extra copies buy redundancy rather than
> speed — you only ever need four Advisors on the battlefield to switch the engine on. 31 is what "as many as possible"
> looks like once the deck still hits its land drops and can pay for its own abilities; 20-24 with more interaction would win more games. Easy to dial back.
>
