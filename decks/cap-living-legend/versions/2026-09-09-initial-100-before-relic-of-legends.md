# Captain America, Living Legend — Azorius Free-Crew Vehicles

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Cyclonic Rift · Enlightened Tutor · Teferi's Protection

> Authoritative current list. Edit alongside STATUS.md. See ../README.md.
>
> **The engine:** every creature you control gets "the first time it becomes tapped during your
> turn, untap it" (CR 603.2e — cause-agnostic, so cost-taps count). That erases every tap cost in
> the deck: Crew is free and your blockers stay up, Station fires TWICE per creature per turn,
> every mana dork taps for double, convoke and improvise cost nothing, and the whole team gets
> pseudo-vigilance (CR 508.2b puts the untap before blockers; CR 506.4b keeps them attacking).
>
> **Two limits that shape the list.** (1) The trigger says "during your turn" — Cap does NOTHING
> on opponents' turns, so the deck must be proactive, and untappers that work on their turns
> (Lita, Unwinding Clock effects) are covering a real hole. (2) The untap always resolves AFTER
> the spell/ability that caused the tap is fully cast (CR 601.2i → 603.3), so the bonus mana can
> never pay for the spell that generated it — tap with priority first, then cast.
>
