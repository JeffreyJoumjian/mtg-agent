# Captain America, Living Legend — Tap Engine (rebuild)

Commander: Captain America, Living Legend (UW)
Bracket: 3   ·   Total: 100/100

Game Changers (3/3 — at the bracket 3 cap): Cyclonic Rift · Enlightened Tutor · Teferi's Protection

> Built from scratch 2026-09-10 to answer the pilot's question: *what is the most reliably consistent
> way to maximise his ability?* **Every creature gets two meaningful taps per turn.** The first tap
> crews a Vehicle or stations a Spacecraft (or feeds Grand Architect, Relic of Legends, convoke);
> Cap untaps it; the second tap uses the creature's own ability — draw, mana, removal, protection,
> tapping a blocker. "Becomes tapped" payoffs (Fallowsage, Tui and La, Mechan Navigator, Sanwell,
> Unctus's loot on every blue creature) fire on BOTH taps, because crewing taps them. Drumbellower and
> Unwinding Clock add a third tap on every opponent's untap step.
>
> The Vehicles and Spacecraft are the win condition: many independent threats, crewed for free,
> creatures only when you choose (sorcery-speed wipes miss them), and the kill doesn't depend on Cap
> surviving. Adept Watershaper / The Wandering Rescuer protect the creatures you leave tapped.
>
> **No infinite loops (audited by mtg-rules-expert, 2026-09-10).** Every payoff needs a tap and every
> untap pays for exactly one tap. The only repeatable cycles are Ioreth ⇄ Thousand-Year Elixir ({1} a
> cycle) and Ioreth ⇄ Minamo ({U} a cycle) — finite mana sinks, one loot each with Unctus. **Do not
> add:** Marvin, Murderous Mimic; a second "untap target permanent" creature (Kelpie Guide, Vizier of
> Tumbling Sands, Aphetto Alchemist); a legendary creature with "{T}: untap"; an untapper that doesn't
> tap itself (Retrofitter Foundry); or anything that adds mana when a permanent taps or untaps. Each
> of those turns the Ioreth circuit infinite.
>
> Sits beside DECK.md (Vehicles) and DECK-COUNTERS.md (lifelink voltron). Grounds: research/decisions.md.
