# The Lord of Pain — Decklist (Bracket 4)

Commander: The Lord of Pain (Rakdos, B/R)
Bracket: 4 (Optimized) · Total: 100 · Research: research/decisions.md

> Authoritative current list for the **Bracket 4** build. Edit alongside STATUS.md.
> Bracket 3 variant: `DECK-B3.md` (derived — see SIDEBOARD.md for the swap list).
>
> **Gameplan:** Rakdos group-slug with a poisoned-gift engine. Hand every opponent extra cards,
> extra mana and free spells (Howling Mine, Font of Mythos, Temple Bell, Seizan, Mana Flare,
> Descent into Avernus) so that they draw more, cast more and
> cast bigger — then bill every one of those actions: each draw (Underworld Dreams, Fate
> Unraveler, Kederekt Parasite, Sheoldred, Razorkin Needlehead, Orcish Bowmasters, Scrawling
> Crawler, Spiteful Visions), each first spell of the turn (The Lord of Pain — you pick who eats
> the damage), every spell (Kaervek, Painful Quandary), every upkeep (Mogis, Seizan), every
> land tapped (Manabarbs). Amplify it with opponent-only boosters (Solphim, Torbran, Torture
> Pit, Twinflame Tyrant, Bloodletter, Wound Reflection) and keep yourself alive on Exquisite Blood /
> Bloodthirsty Conqueror / Sheoldred while the commander stops them gaining anything back.
> Close with wheels into the draw-punishers, Torment of Hailfire / Exsanguinate / Gray Merchant,
> Heartless Hidetsugu under a doubler, or the Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond loop.
> Your own damage comes back as life: Basilisk Collar on the commander, Whip of Erebos on every
> pinger, and Glacial Chasm prevents all damage dealt to you.
