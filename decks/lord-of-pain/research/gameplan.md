# The Lord of Pain — Gameplan (for the pilot)

## What the deck is

A Rakdos group-slug deck that *looks* like group hug. You hand the table extra cards, extra mana,
free Treasures and a fresh hand every turn — and every one of those gifts is billed: each draw,
each spell, each upkeep, each land tapped. The commander is the cashier: every player's first
spell each turn deals its mana value to **a player you choose** (anyone but the caster), and
nobody on the other side can gain life back.

## Mulligan

**Keep** a 7 with 3+ lands (or 2 lands + a rock/Medallion) **and** at least one of: a draw-punisher
(Kederekt Parasite, Razorkin Needlehead, Underworld Dreams, Sheoldred, Fate Unraveler,
Bowmasters), a gift engine (Howling Mine, Spiteful Visions, Font, Temple Bell), or a
Medallion. One early play is enough; two is a snap-keep.

**The dream 7:** land, land, Sol Ring or a Medallion, Kederekt Parasite or Razorkin, Spiteful Visions
or Howling Mine, one removal/protection spell, anything. That curves rock → 2-drop punisher →
Lord on 3 → gift on 4, and the bill starts on turn 4.

**The fast-mana openers (added 2026-09-22).** T1 Mana Vault off any land puts the **Lord on turn 2**
(2 lands + {C}{C}{C}). T2 Grim Monolith gets there on 3 with your land drop still up. Mox Diamond
wants a land in hand — at 34 lands that is usually fine, but don't keep a 2-lander on the strength of
it. Dark Ritual is best spent on a 4-drop engine piece two turns early, not held for a finisher.
All the self-damage (Vault's draw-step 1, Ancient Tomb's 2) is **erased by Glacial Chasm** while the
mana still happens — CR 615.6 for the prevention, 605.3b for Tomb's mana ability resolving anyway.

**Ship it** if: 1 land; 5+ lands with no engine piece; or a hand that is all amplifiers/finishers
(Torbran, Solphim, Wound Reflection, Torment, Hidetsugu) with no punisher — those cards do nothing
until the punishers are down. Amplifiers are the *second* thing you deploy, never the first.

**6-card keeps** are fine with 2 lands + rock + one engine piece; this deck draws 3+ extra a turn
once an engine is out, so a slightly light hand recovers.

## Tutors — what to get (Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor)

Ask, in order: *what is missing from [punisher + gift + protection]?* Fetch the missing third.

| Board state | Get | Why |
|---|---|---|
| No draw-punisher down yet | **Sheoldred, the Apocalypse** | Best single card: punishes, and pays you 2 per draw (the lifeline). Second choice Spiteful Visions (gift + punish in one). |
| Punishers down, no gift | **Font of Mythos** (or Howling Mine if mana-tight) | Turns 1 draw a turn into 3 — triples every punisher. |
| Punishers + gift down, table is healthy | **Solphim, Mayhem Dominus** | Doubles everything noncombat; the first amplifier is the one that matters. |
| Commander keeps dying / removal-heavy pod | **Mithril Coat** (flash, indestructible) or Kaya's Ghostform | The engine's single point of failure is the Lord. |
| Under attack / going wide at you | **No Mercy** or **Toxic Deluge** / Blasphemous Act | Stabilise first; the engine wins the long game. |
| Exquisite Blood (or Conqueror) already out, any drain live | **Sanguine Bond** | Ends the game on the next point of life lost. The reverse (Bond out → get Exquisite) is the same. |
| A doubler is out (Solphim / Twinflame) | **Heartless Hidetsugu** | Every even life total dies, odd go to 1; add Torbran and everyone dies. |
| Bloodletter out, 9 mana available | **Fraying Omnipotence** | Each opponent loses their whole life total. |
| Punishers out, big mana turn coming | **Wheel of Fortune** | 7 draws × punishers per opponent. Cast Bloodletter first. |
| Flooding on mana, nothing to do | **Torment of Hailfire** (with Cabal Coffers/Urborg) | X × 3 per opponent, 6 under Bloodletter. |

Vampiric Tutor puts the card on top: cast it in your end step (or in response to a Teferi's Puzzle
Box trigger — the normal draw happens before draw-step triggers, so a tutored card gets bottomed
otherwise). Never tutor an amplifier before a punisher, and never tutor a gift before a punisher —
the gift alone is pure group hug.

## Sequencing

1. **T1–2:** rock / Medallion / Kederekt / Bloodchief / Razorkin. Kederekt needs a red permanent:
   a Medallion-less T1 Parasite comes online with Razorkin, Spiteful, the Lord, Torbran, Solphim.
2. **T3:** The Lord under both Medallions, or (Tomb/Sol Ring) on curve at 5. Cast the commander
   **before** the first symmetric gift if you can — the gift is only a gift once the bill exists.
3. **Order of gifts:** Spiteful Visions first (it punishes as it gives), then Howling Mine / Font /
   Temple Bell once two draw-punishers are down. **Teferi's Puzzle Box last** — only with ≥2
   draw-punishers out, ideally Sheoldred or Exquisite Blood too (it shuffles your own hand every
   turn and Spiteful pings you per draw; Sheoldred pays you 2 per draw).
4. **Amplifiers after payoffs.** Solphim/Torbran/Torture Pit with nothing on board are blanks; with
   three punishers out each one roughly doubles the clock. Bloodletter is for **your** turn (wheels,
   Torment, Exsanguinate, Fraying Omnipotence); Wound Reflection is for theirs.
5. **Temple Bell** timing: tap it in an opponent's end step (they draw into Sheoldred/Dreams with
   no main phase to use the card) or in response to a wipe.
6. **Kaervek** is the biggest single swing after the Lord — it is 5 under both Medallions. Point
   its damage at creatures when a blocker matters and at faces otherwise.

## Where to aim the Lord

- The trigger targets "another" player than the caster: when Alice casts, hit Bob or Carol; when
  you cast, hit whoever is ahead. Use it to **keep the table even** — the deck wins long games.
- Opponents with Leyline of Sanctity / Aegis of the Gods / Shalai can't be targeted by it — kill
  the enchantment (Feed the Swarm, Chaos Warp) or route damage to the others.
- Your own first spell each turn triggers it too (and Vial Smasher at a random opponent): cast a
  cheap spell precombat every turn even if it is just a Signet — 2 damage is 6 under amplifiers.

## Staying alive

- Exquisite Blood / Bloodthirsty Conqueror: every point the table loses comes back to you. Under
  either, the symmetric pieces (Spiteful Visions, Descent, Manabarbs, Seizan, Hidetsugu) are net
  positive for you.
- Sheoldred +2 per card you draw; under Mine+Font that is +6 a turn.
- Basilisk Collar on the commander from the turn it lands (1 + equip 2): every trigger is life. Whip of
  Erebos makes every pinger lifelink — under Whip, Heartless Hidetsugu is a net lifegain for you.
- Glacial Chasm: drop it when the table turns on you or once 3+ symmetric pieces are out; sacrifice a
  tapped/colourless land to it. Pay 2, 4, 6… — let it go when the upkeep outgrows the prevention.
- Witch's Clinic: {2} to give the commander lifelink for a turn if Collar is gone.
- No Mercy + a 5/5 menace blocker. The table *will* decide you are the problem around turn 5 —
  hold Deflecting Swat / Mithril Coat for the commander, not for a punisher. **Note the No Mercy /
  Glacial Chasm anti-synergy:** under a Chasm no creature ever deals damage to you, so No Mercy
  never triggers (CR 615.6, 603.2g). Pick one shield per board state; don't rely on both at once.
- Exile-based graveyard hate is **Rakdos Charm** only (Bojuka Bog went pocket 2026-09-22); bring the
  Bog back for a known reanimator pod.

## Kill lines (in order of how often they happen)

1. **Attrition** — three punishers + Font/Mine: ~12 per opponent per turn, doubled by the first
   amplifier, doubled again at end step by Wound Reflection. Most games end here by turn 8–9.
2. **Wheel turn** — Wheel of Fortune with the punishers out: 7 draws × punishers per opponent. Cast
   Bloodletter first.
3. **Heartless Hidetsugu** + Solphim/Twinflame (even totals die, odd go to 1) or + Torbran as well
   (everyone dies). Haste via Swiftfoot Boots.
4. **Bloodletter + Fraying Omnipotence** — each opponent loses half ×2 = all. 9 mana.
5. **Torment of Hailfire / Exsanguinate** with Cabal Coffers/Jeska's Will/Monolith mana; Gray
   Merchant for 10+ each.
6. **Exquisite Blood + Sanguine Bond** (B4) — tutor the second half with Demonic/Vampiric once the
   first is down and any drain is live.

## What beats you

- **Enchantment wipes / Farewell** — the engine is enchantments and artifacts. Don't overextend
  punishers into a known Farewell player; two punishers + one amplifier is enough.
- **Leyline of Sanctity** (you can't target them), **counterspells on the commander** (Hexing
  Squelcher, Swat), **Torpor Orb-style stax** (Font/Mine don't care).
- **Aggro turning on you early** — No Mercy or Glacial Chasm (not both — see above), keep Deadly Rollick/Terminate for the biggest
  attacker, and remember the Lord's trigger can point the caster's own spell damage at whoever is
  attacking you.
- **Your own symmetric pieces** — never run a symmetric multiplier (Fiery Emancipation etc.), never
  a "players can't gain life" card; decisions.md explains why.
