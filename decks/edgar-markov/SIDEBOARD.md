| Card | Cost | What it does | Why cut | Replaced by | Own |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## D. Watch list — evaluated 2026-08-03, not yet slotted

Full reasoning in `research/swaps.md`. Ranked within tier.

### Tier 1 — expected Phase 2 includes
- **Emeritus of Woe // Demonic Tutor** `{3}{B}` — 5/4 **Vampire**; enters "prepared" → cast a copy of
  Demonic Tutor; **re-prepares whenever 2+ creatures died that turn**. Repeatable tutor on a body,
  and **not on the Game Changer list** (plain Demonic Tutor is). $12 vs $72.
- **Urza's Incubator** `{3}` — Vampire spells cost **{2}** less. Stacks with Herald's Horn.
- **Master of Dark Rites** `{B}` — 1/1 Vampire. `{T}`, sac another creature: add `{B}{B}{B}`
  (Vampire/Cleric/Demon only). 1-mana sac outlet **and** ritual.
- **Warleader's Call** `{1}{R}{W}` — team +1/+1 **and** creature enters → 1 damage to each opponent.
  Impact Tremors with an anthem stapled on.
- **Phyrexian Reclamation** `{B}` — `{1}{B}`, pay 2 life: creature from graveyard → **hand**.
  Repeatable. Returning to hand means re-casting it, which **re-triggers eminence**.
- **Blade of the Bloodchief** `{1}` — creature dies → +1/+1 counter on equipped; **two** if it's a
  Vampire. Counts *any* death, including opponents'.
- **Vault of the Archangel** (land) — `{2}{W}{B}`, `{T}`: team gains deathtouch **and lifelink**.
  Free roll in the manabase; directly rebuilds the life floor.
- **Phyrexian Tower** (land) — `{T}`, sac a creature: add `{B}{B}`. Free sac outlet on a land.
- **Three Tree City** (land) — `{2}`,`{T}`: mana equal to your Vampire count.

### Tier 2 — strong, needs a slot fight
- **Plumb the Forbidden** `{1}{B}` — sac any number of creatures, copy per creature, draw 1 / lose 1
  each. **The anti-wipe card**: in response to a wrath, sac the board and draw 6-8.
- **Village Rites** `{B}` / **Deadly Dispute** `{1}{B}` — sac, draw 2 (Dispute adds a Treasure).
- **Diabolic Intent** `{1}{B}` — sac a creature, tutor. **Not a Game Changer.** The sac is upside here.
- **Florian, Voldaren Scion** `{1}{B}{R}` — 3/3 first strike; postcombat, look at top X where X =
  life opponents lost this turn, exile one, may play it **that turn**. X is routinely 8+ here.
- **March of the Canonized** `{X}{W}{W}` — X 1/1 **lifelink** Vampire tokens; upkeep 4/3 flier at
  devotion 7+. Elspeth doubles the tokens.
- **Glass-Cast Heart** `{2}{B}` — Blood on Vampire attack; `{B}`,`{T}`, pay 1: 1/1 lifelink Vampire token.
- **Door of Destinies** `{4}` — counter per Vampire **cast**, +1/+1 each. Quadratic; nothing on turn 1.
- **Patchwork Banner** `{3}` — Vampires +1/+1 **and** taps for any color.
- **Creeping Bloodsucker** `{1}{B}` — upkeep: 1 damage to each opponent, gain that much.
- **Forerunner of the Legion** `{2}{W}` — ETB tutor a Vampire to the **top** (not hand).
- **Patron of the Vein** `{4}{B}{B}` — ETB destroy a creature; opponents' creatures that die are
  **exiled** and every Vampire gets a counter. Removal + graveyard hate + pump, but 6 mana.
- **And They Shall Know No Fear** `{1}{W}` — chosen type gets +1/+0 and **indestructible**, instant.

### Tier 3 — real cards, wrong deck or wrong cost
- **Phyrexian Altar** `{3}` $56 — sac: any color mana. Great, but expensive and outlets are capped.
- **Eldrazi Monument** `{5}` — +1/+1, flying, indestructible; sac a creature each upkeep.
- **Banner of Kinship** `{5}` — static snapshot at ETB, doesn't grow. Door of Destinies is better late.
- **Vanquisher's Banner** `{5}` — anthem + draw on Vampire cast. 5 mana colorless is clunky.
- **All-Out Assault** `{2}{R}{W}{B}` — +1/+1, deathtouch, **extra combat**. Finisher, win-more.
- **Rush of Dread** `{1}{B}{B}` + spree — sac half / discard half / lose half life. **Single opponent**,
  and all three modes is 8 mana.
- **Descendants' Fury** `{3}{R}` — sac on combat damage → cheat a shared-type creature into play. Random.
- **Dread Return** `{2}{B}{B}` — reanimate; flashback sac 3.
- **Westvale Abbey** (land) — flip needs 5 creatures + 5 mana. Colorless land in a 3-colour deck.
- **Talisman of Conviction** `{2}` — completes the talisman trio; already at 9 rocks.
- **Butcher of Malakir** `{5}{B}{B}` — Grave Pact on a 7-drop body. Dictate of Erebos already does it.

### Tier 4 — pass, with reason
- **Patriarch's Bidding** `{3}{B}{B}` — **symmetrical**; every opponent reanimates their tribe too.
- **Bolas's Citadel** ⭐ **Game Changer** — pay life to cast off the top. Costs a bracket slot *and*
  attacks the life total this deck is already short on.
- **Nighthawk Scavenger** — requires tracking opponents' graveyards. **Pilot veto.**
- **Kalastria Highborn** — needs `{B}` per trigger; blank when wiped and tapped out. Rejected.
- **Vraan, Executioner Thane** — once per turn in a mass-death deck. Rejected.
