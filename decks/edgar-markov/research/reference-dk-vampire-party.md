# DK's Vampire Party! — reference list

Edgar Markov. 100 cards. Reconstructed from `research/new_edgar_decks.json`
(card names only — the original Moxfield URL was not preserved when this was scraped).

Why it's here: it is the **only no-infinite-combo list** in the 6-deck premium sample, has the
highest lifegain (24) and lifelink (16) density of any of them, and independently runs nearly
every card we landed on this session. Used as the build template, not to be copied.

`♥` = lifelink.  `← not in yours` = missing from the current DECK.md.
