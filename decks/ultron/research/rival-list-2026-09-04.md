## What his list is

A **Tron ramp shell with a loop package**, not a copy deck: Basalt Monolith + Rings of
Brighthearth (infinite colourless), Basalt + Forsaken Monument (infinite), Krark-Clan Ironworks +
Myr Retriever + Junk Diver + Scrap Trawler (loops), Ashnod's Altar, Mycosynth Lattice + Karn, the
Great Creator (every opponent permanent becomes an artifact whose abilities can't be activated —
including lands), Aetherflux Reservoir and Walking Ballista as outlets. Three Game Changers (Mana
Vault, Ancient Tomb, The One Ring) so the count says 3; the two-card infinites and the Lattice
lock are what our founding brief excluded. He runs one copy engine we don't (none) and skips six
of ours (Mirrorworks, Prototype Portal, Mirror Box, Cybermen Squadron, Chief, Machine Overlord).

## Not borrowing, with grounds

- **Basalt Monolith, Rings of Brighthearth, Krark-Clan Ironworks, Ashnod's Altar, Myr Retriever,
  Junk Diver, Mycosynth Lattice, Karn the Great Creator (as a lock), Aetherflux Reservoir** — the
  loop package. Excluded 2026-09-03 on the Bracket 3 brief; nothing has changed.
- **Mana Vault** — Game Changer cap; Mishra's Workshop holds the third slot.
- **Ugin, the Spirit Dragon** ({8}) — a fourth coloured-permanent wipe behind All Is Dust and
  both Ugins already in; the curve is the cost.
- **Calamity of the Titans** — exiles every creature below the revealed card's MV, ours included;
  Darksteel Forge doesn't stop exile.
- **Rise of the Eldrazi** ({12}, one extra turn) — legal at Bracket 3 (not chained) but twelve mana
  for one turn in a deck that wins by having a board.
- **Selective Obliteration, Desecrate Reality, Spatial Contortion** — fine cards; our nine
  interaction slots already hold the one-sided wipe (All Is Dust) and the repeatable exile (Ugin
  EotS). Spatial Contortion is the sideboard pick against dork-heavy pods.
- **Nettlecyst, Shadowspear** — voltron pieces; not the brief.
- **Sensei's Divining Top, Worn Powerstone, Voltaic Key, Shimmer Myr, Zhalfirin Void, Hall of
  Tagsin, Foundry of the Consuls, Urza's Factory, Secluded Starforge, Scene of the Crime, Plaza of
  Heroes, Homeward Path, Dust Bowl, Desert** — each buys a little; none buys more than the card in
  its slot. Secluded Starforge's 2/2 Robot token and Plaza's one-shot hexproof for Ultron are the
  two closest.
