# Ultron — Sideboard

Authoritative sideboard. The deck is at exactly 100, so every card that comes in names the card it
displaces. Grounds for each are in `research/decisions.md` (2026-09-03).

| Card | Bring in when | Displaces |
|---|---|---|
| [Basalt Monolith](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Basalt+Monolith%22) | Bracket 4 table only — infinite colourless with Forsaken Monument | Worn Powerstone |
| [Mana Vault](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Mana+Vault%22) | Bracket 4 (fourth Game Changer) | Hedron Archive |
| [Eldritch Immunity](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Eldritch+Immunity%22) | Coloured sweepers (Blasphemous Act, Toxic Deluge) or a wall of coloured blockers | Kozilek's Command |
| [Spine of Ish Sah](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Spine+of+Ish+Sah%22) | Pods full of enchantments and planeswalkers the creature-removal misses | Duplicant |
| [Tezzeret, Cruel Captain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Tezzeret%2C+Cruel+Captain%22) | Games are being lost to mana; slow pods without fliers | Solemn Simulacrum |
| [Karn, the Great Creator](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Karn%2C+the+Great+Creator%22) | Artifact-heavy or Treasure-heavy pod (it shuts their activations off) | Null Elemental Blast |
| [Metalwork Colossus](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Metalwork+Colossus%22) | The deck wants a free 10/10 in the top end | Duplicant |
| [Coveted Jewel](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Coveted+Jewel%22) | Pods that don't attack (draw-go, combo) | Iron Spider, Stark Upgrade |
| [Myr Retriever](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Myr+Retriever%22) | Graveyard-hate-free pods where recursion matters more than a body | Palladium Myr |
| [Scrap Trawler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Scrap+Trawler%22) | Hangarback Walker underperforms — recursion over a mana sink (swapped out 2026-09-16) | Hangarback Walker |
| [Blast Zone](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Blast+Zone%22) | Token decks (X=0 clears every token on the table, ours included) | one Wastes |
