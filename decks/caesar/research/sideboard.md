# Caesar, Legion's Emperor: sideboard

Every row names the card it displaces in `main`, so a swap is never ambiguous. `deck.json` is the
list; this file is prose about it.

| Card | Bring it in when | Displaces |
|---|---|---|
| [Vengeance Mission](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Vengeance+Mission%22) | Held 2026-11-14 (Star Trek review). With Grave Pact or Dictate of Erebos out, each of your deaths triggers it four times, and from seven quest counters each trigger drains 2 from one opponent. It's an enchantment, so it survives creature wipes. | [Cruel Celebrant](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Cruel+Celebrant%22) |
| [The Needs of the Many](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22The+Needs+of+the+Many%22) | A pod with lots of wipes or a combat kill aimed at you, and you want a second Teferi's Protection-style save. For {1}{W} and a sacrificed creature, your creatures get hexproof and indestructible, you get hexproof, and all damage to you is prevented this turn. The pilot asked to keep it in mind (2026-11-14). | [Flawless Maneuver](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Flawless+Maneuver%22) |
| [Hero of Bladehold](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Hero+of+Bladehold%22) | Cut 2026-11-14 for Worf. Bring it back if Worf's missing haste costs too many attacks, or you want battle cry's pump on attacking Tribbles. | [Worf, Still Not Merry](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Worf%2C+Still+Not+Merry%22) |
| [Assemble the Legion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Assemble+the+Legion%22) | Cut 2026-11-14 for the Tribbles. Bring it back if creature wipes keep resetting the Tribbles, because Assemble survives them. | [Troubling Tribbles](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Troubling+Tribbles%22) |
| [Lich's Relic](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Lich's+Relic%22) | Cut 2026-11-14 for Heraldic Banner. Bring it back if you need more targeted removal than ramp. | [Heraldic Banner](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Heraldic+Banner%22) |
| [Tocasia's Welcome](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Tocasia's+Welcome%22) | Cut 2026-11-14 for Caretaker's Talent. | [Zurgo Stormrender](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Zurgo+Stormrender%22) | Cut 2026-11-15 for Mirkwood Bats (lifegain audit). Bring it back if you miss its draw when attacking tokens leave. | [Mirkwood Bats](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Mirkwood+Bats%22) |
| [Swords to Plowshares](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Swords+to+Plowshares%22) | Cut 2026-11-15 for South Wind Avatar (the pilot's pick). Bring it back if four removal spells isn't enough.
