# Scarlet Witch — Decklist (Bracket 3-high)

Commander: The Scarlet Witch (mono-red)
Bracket: 3 (high) · Game Changers: 3/3 · Total: 100 · Research: research/sample-deck-analysis.md

> Authoritative current list for the **Bracket 3** build. Edit alongside STATUS.md.
> Sideboard: `SIDEBOARD.md`. Bracket 4 variant: `DECK-B4.md`.
>
> **Gameplan:** ramp and ritual into instants/sorceries of mana value 4+, which The Scarlet Witch
> discounts by her power. Grow her power mid-turn (Livaan, Cait Sith, Blazing Shoal) so each
> haymaker makes the next one cheaper, then close with Crackle with Power, Apex of Power, or
> Chandra's Ignition pointed at a pumped commander.
>
> **Sequencing note:** on a Neheb turn, burn in your *precombat* main, then collect {R} per point
> of life your opponents lost at the start of your *postcombat* main and cast the X-spell there.
> The postcombat main phase happens whether or not you attack (rule 500.1).

**Game Changers (3/3):** Ancient Tomb · Jeska's Will · The One Ring

**Bracket 3 compliance:** no mass land denial (Demolition Field is single-target), no extra-turn
cards, no two-card infinite combos. Reiterate is deliberately held to the Bracket 4 list — with
Mana Geyser it makes infinite mana, and that argument isn't worth having at an LGS.

**Finalized 2026-08-02.** The damage-multiplier question resolved in favour of Solphim (4 mana,
doubles only damage aimed at opponents) over Fiery Emancipation (6 mana, triples everything
including damage to your own board). One multiplier is right; two is greedy.

**No unconditional wrath — this is deliberate.** Blasphemous Act was cut for Mithril Coat and sits
in the sideboard. The three sweepers left all spare the commander: Volcanic Vision is one-sided,
Chandra's Ignition hits "each *other* creature," and Fiery Confluence does 3. Blasphemous Act was
the only one that killed Wanda, and the deck's whole mana math runs through her. Board it back in
against tables with large creatures, where Ignition at 2 power and Confluence at 3 don't get there.

# Sideboard (20) — swaps, and why each one left

The deck is at exactly 100, so every card you bring in needs the matching card to come out.
Entries deliberately avoid a leading count so the card tool doesn't parse them as deck cards.

## Swap in for a specific table

- **Blasphemous Act** — in for *Volcanic Vision*.
  *Why it left:* the only sweeper that kills Wanda, and the deck's mana math runs through her.
  *Bring in vs:* boards with large creatures, where Chandra's Ignition at 2 power and Fiery
  Confluence at 3 don't get there. Costs {1} less per creature on the battlefield, so often {R}.
- **Rousing Refrain** — in for *Mana Geyser*.
  *Why it left:* table-dependent — it scales off your opponents' hand sizes.
  *Bring in vs:* control, counterspell decks, Rhystic Study / Mystic Remora pods. Three opponents
  holding four cards each is 12 red mana. Dead against aggro.
- **Insurrection** — in for *Chandra's Ignition*.
  *Why it left:* 6 mana after the discount, wins through **combat**, and is blank against a
  creature-light table. It is **not** a defensive card — it's a sorcery, so it can't answer an attack.
  *Bring in vs:* creature-heavy boards worth stealing.
- **Swiftfoot Boots** — in for *Champion's Helm*.
  *Why it left:* redundant. Hexproof doesn't stack, and haste is worthless here — Wanda's discount
  is a static ability that works the moment she resolves.
  *Bring in vs:* artifact-removal-heavy pods, or if you want haste for the Blackblade line.
- **Darksteel Plate** — in for *Return the Favor*.
  *Why it left:* five mana all-in and no flash, so it's strictly worse than Mithril Coat.
  *Bring in vs:* removal-heavy pods where you want a second indestructible source.
- **Nova Flame** — in for *Volcanic Vision*.
  *Why it left:* no damage to players, so it can't close a game.
  *Bring in vs:* creature decks. The +1/+1 counters on Wanda are permanent, so the discount grows.
- **Prisoner's Dilemma** — in for *Fiery Confluence*.
  *Why it left:* your **opponents** choose the outcome, and this deck kills deterministically.
  *Bring in vs:* pods that enjoy the politics.
- **Pinnacle Monk // Mystic Peak** — in for *a Mountain*.
  *Why it left:* the spell half is a **creature**, so Wanda never discounts it, and Mystic Peak
  isn't a Mountain — it turns off Valakut and adds no Nykthos devotion.
  *Bring in vs:* nothing specific — bring it in if you keep flooding or getting land-screwed.

## Lost a slot on rate, not on quality

- **Hit the Mother Lode** — in for *Tablet of Discovery*.
  *Why it left:* its Treasures enter **tapped**, so the mana is for next turn, and Brass's Bounty
  makes about 14 **untapped** Treasures for the same 5 mana after the discount.
- **Pirate's Pillage** — in for *Big Score*.
  *Why it left:* word-for-word identical to Big Score at the same cost, but a **sorcery** instead of
  an instant. The third copy of an effect the deck already runs twice.
- **Fiery Emancipation** — in for *Solphim, Mayhem Dominus*.
  *Why it left:* six mana, an enchantment so no discount, and it triples damage to **your own**
  board too — Blasphemous Act would read 39 to each creature.
- **Comet Storm** — in for *Electrodominance*.
  *Why it left:* redundant with Crackle with Power.
- **Call Forth the Tempest** — in for *Apex of Power*.
  *Why it left:* eight mana and the cascade is random.
- **Wild Ricochet** — in for *Untimely Malfunction*.
  *Why it left:* the fourth redirect behind Deflecting Swat, Bolt Bend and Return the Favor.
- **Double Vision** — in for *Repeated Reverberation*.
  *Why it left:* five mana to copy a single spell per turn.
- **Blazing Crescendo** — in for *Monstrous Rage*.
  *Why it left:* outclassed — Monstrous Rage is the same +3/+1 for half the mana and leaves a
  permanent +1/+1 Role.
- **Witch's Mark** — in for *Monstrous Rage*.
  *Why it left:* mana value 2, so no discount. The Wicked Role is a permanent +1 to Wanda's power.
- **Coruscation Mage** — in for *Fiery Inscription*.
  *Why it left:* chip damage on a 2/2 in a deck that wants one big turn. Offspring {2} gives a
  second body.
- **Molten-Core Maestro** — in for *Storm-Kiln Artist*.
  *Why it left:* its mana ability needs 5+ mana **spent**, and only 6 of 37 fixed-cost spells still
  cost that much after a 2-power discount. Wanda's discount actively turns it off.
- **Fellwar Stone** — in for *Mind Stone*.
  *Why it left:* in mono-red it often can't produce {R} at all, and we have {R}{R} and {R}{R}{R}
  costs throughout. A build error on my part.

## Rules gotchas

- **Free-casting an X-spell forces X = 0** (rule 107.3b). Never point Improvisation
  Capstone, Mizzix's Mastery, Electrodominance or Hit the Mother Lode's discover at Crackle with
  Power or Storm King's Thunder. Wanda's cost *reduction* is fine — only free-casting breaks it.
- **Neheb turns are two-stage** (rule 500.1). The postcombat main phase happens whether or not you
  attack. Burn precombat, collect {R} per point of life lost postcombat, cast the X-spell there.
- **Hexproof and protection both fail against wraths** — a wrath neither targets nor deals damage.
  Your real answers are Mithril Coat held up at instant speed, and Tyrite Sanctum's indestructible
  counter.
