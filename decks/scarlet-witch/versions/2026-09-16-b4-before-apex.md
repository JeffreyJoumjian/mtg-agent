# Scarlet Witch — Decklist B4 (the chain build, unrestricted)

Commander: The Scarlet Witch (mono-red)
Bracket: 4 (Optimized) · Game Changers: 10 (no cap at B4) · Total: 100

> **Built 2026-09-08.** This is `DECK-V3.md` with the Game Changer cap lifted, not a different
> deck. The plan is identical — Livaan plus an X-spell doubles Wanda for her coloured pips, and
> the chain compounds (`research/turn-5-chain-2026-09-08.md`). Bracket 4 does not make the chain
> bigger. **It makes it earlier and more reliable:** turn 3–4 instead of turn 5–6.
>
> The previous Bracket 4 list, which was the *grind* deck with a fast manabase bolted on, is
> retired at `versions/2026-09-08-b4-retired.md`.
>
> **The honest headline: most Bracket 4 power is colourless, and this deck spends red pips.**
> Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Ancient Tomb and City of Traitors buy *deployment speed* — Wanda on
> turn 1–2, Livaan on turn 2 — and contribute nothing to chain length. The cards that genuinely
> lengthen the chain are the ones that make **red**: Lotus Petal, Mox Amber, Chrome Mox, Mox
> Diamond and Lion's Eye Diamond, plus Rite of Flame as a fourth ritual and **Explosive Welcome**,
> which is the only card in the deck that turns colourless mana *into* red while dealing 8 damage.
> Underworld Breach is the one card here that makes the *kill itself* better.
>
> **Not included, and not by oversight:** Mana Crypt and Jeweled Lotus are **banned** in Commander.
> Panoptic Mirror free-casts, which forces X = 0. Field of the Dead wants many differently named
> lands and this runs 18 Mountains. Mishra's Workshop only pays for artifacts. The Tabernacle at
> Pendrell Vale taxes Wanda, Livaan and every engine in the list.

**What Bracket 4 actually buys, by axis:**

| Axis | Cards | Effect on the plan |
|---|---|---|
| **Deployment speed** | Ancient Tomb, City of Traitors, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Sol Ring | Wanda turn 1–2, Livaan turn 2–3. Colourless, so **zero** contribution to chain length. |
| **Red pips** | Lotus Petal, Mox Amber, Chrome Mox, Mox Diamond, Lion's Eye Diamond, Rite of Flame, Explosive Welcome | The only axis that makes the chain *longer*. Lion's Eye Diamond is three red at instant speed for a hand you have already emptied; Explosive Welcome is net +2 red **and** a spell, so it feeds every trigger on the way past. |
| **A second chain** | Underworld Breach, Reiterate | Breach's escape cost is the card's mana cost plus exiling three, and that **includes X** (CR 107.3b), so a recurred Storm King's Thunder or Jaya's is cast at a real X. |
| **Consistency** | The One Ring, Faithless Looting, Gamble | Finds Livaan or Storm King's Thunder. Mono-red's to-hand tutor pool is two cards and one of them is a Game Changer. |

**Loops in this list, stated plainly.** Reiterate with buyback copying Mana Geyser, and Lion's Eye
Diamond escaped off Underworld Breach, are both **chosen-N** loops — the pilot announces each
iteration and the graveyard bounds it. They are legal at Bracket 4 without qualification and, by
the pilot's own 2026-09-06 line, are not "automatic or unstoppable" infinites either. Nothing here
loops without a decision point.

**What was cut from V3 to make room (16):** Nykthos, Rogue's Passage and 3 Mountains (28 lands,
not 32) · Urabrask and The Vision and Scarlet Witch (cast-only engines that do not scale with
copies) · Blackblade Reforged and Gauntlet of Power (both scale with lands or cost 5; neither is
live on turn 3) · Fiery Confluence · Twinferno · Fists of Flame · Reforge the Soul.

## Rules gotchas that are new here

- **Neheb is a damage-to-mana converter, not late mana.** *"At the beginning of each of your
  postcombat main phases, add {R} for each 1 life your opponents have lost this turn"* — the
  combined total across all opponents, and damage to a player **is** life loss (CR 120.3a). A
  precombat main that deals 21 to each of three opponents is 63 life lost, so Neheb hands you
  **63 red** for a second main phase in the same turn. The postcombat main happens whether or not
  you attack (CR 500.1), Jaya's and Crackle are sorceries so they are castable there, and Past in
  Flames re-casts them at a real X. **This is the recovery line when the chain falls short** — at
  the pip floor a seed-2 chain is 21 to each opponent, which is not lethal, and the second main
  phase kills. Whatever you do not spend banks under Electro or Ashling. He must already be on the
  battlefield when the postcombat main begins, so he is a turn-before play, never mid-chain.
- **Birgi triggers on ANY spell**, not only instants and sorceries — every mox, every Equipment,
  Underworld Breach, all of it — and her mana carries its own *"until end of turn you don't lose
  this mana"* clause, so it does not need Electro or Ashling to survive the phase. She is a modal
  DFC: cast **Harnfel, Horn of Bounty** off the back instead when you are flooded on mana and short
  on cards (discard a card, exile the top two, play them this turn).
- **Arcane Bombardment triggers on EVERY turn, not every turn cycle.** *"Whenever you cast your
  first instant or sorcery spell each turn"* reads each turn of the game, so a cheap instant on
  each opponent's turn fires it again — up to **four triggers a turn cycle** in a four-player pod
  (LEDGER 2026-08-23). Deflecting Swat is free with a commander out and is the ideal trigger.
  Each trigger exiles one more card and then recopies the **whole** pile, so output is turn count
  times pile size, not one copy (LEDGER 2026-08-19). **The copies free-cast the rituals**, which
  is the point in this deck: a Seething Song in the pile is five red pips every trigger, and red
  pips are what limits the chain. The cost of the same clause is that free casting forces X = 0
  (CR 107.3b), so an exiled X-spell is a blank — about one card in four here.
- **Lion's Eye Diamond's discard is a cost**, paid on activation, and it is instant speed. On the
  chain turn your hand is usually spent anyway, so it is three red pips for nothing. Do **not**
  crack it with cards you still intend to cast — and note it does not help a hand you are holding
  Deflecting Swat in.
- **Chrome Mox and Mox Diamond cost a card and a land** respectively. In a deck that wants land
  drops for Valakut and needs cards for the chain, they are not free — they are red pips bought
  with cards.
- **Mox Amber only taps once a legendary is out** — Wanda, Livaan, Thor, Longshot, Electro or
  Ashling. It is blank in an opening hand with none of them.
- **Explosive Welcome is a mana source that is also a spell**, which is the whole reason it is here
  over a free-mana card like Simian Spirit Guide. `{7}{R}`, mana value **8**, so Wanda discounts it
  hard: with her at 4 plus Ruby Medallion and Longshot the reduction is 6 and it costs `{1}{R}`,
  then it *adds* `{R}{R}{R}` — **net +2 red for two mana**, converting colourless into red, which
  is the deck's actual bottleneck. Mana value 8 also makes it the single biggest Livaan trigger in
  the list, and being an instant it feeds Storm-Kiln, Ashling, Arcane Bombardment, Thor and
  Longshot on the way past. Its two damage instances must hit **different** targets.
  Sequencing: cast it *before* the expensive links, not after — the three red it adds is what pays
  for them.
- **Underworld Breach exiles three cards from your graveyard per escape**, which shrinks
  Runechanter's Pike and competes with Past in Flames and Will of the Jeskai for the same yard.
- **Mana Vault and Grim Monolith do not untap normally.** Vault deals you 1 per upkeep until paid
  for; both are one explosive turn, which is the turn this deck is playing.
- Everything in `DECK-V3.md`'s gotchas still applies — the MV-4 gate on Wanda's discount, Livaan
  reading noncreature spells only, copies not being cast, Solphim doubling Chandra's Ignition
  before dying to it, and the total cost locking in at CR 601.2f.
