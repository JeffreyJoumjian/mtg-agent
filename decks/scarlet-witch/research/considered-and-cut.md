Every card that appears in **2 or more** of the 11 Scarlet Witch sample decks but is **not** in
`DECK.md` or `DECK-B4.md`, with why. This is the agenda for the card-by-card pass — argue with any
of it. Computed, not recalled: the list comes from diffing the sample data against both builds.

Frequency is out of the 11 Scarlet Witch decks in `../samples/`.

## Cut on principle: the pinger package

Frequency is high because two of the sample decks are *built* on this plan. It's a different deck —
a slow chip clock — and it dies to one board wipe. Our deck wants one big turn instead.

**Guttersnipe** (5/11) · **Coruscation Mage** (3/11) · **Firebrand Archer** (2/11) ·
Electrostatic Field · Erebor Flamesmith · Thunderdrum Soloist

Kept the one exception: **Longshot, Rebel Bowman**, because it also reduces every noncreature
spell by {1}, which the pure pingers don't.

## Cut on principle: one-shot combat pump

The pump-for-discount plan only pays if the pump is free or comes with a card. These cost a card to
add +2 or +3 power for one turn, which is roughly one extra discount — a bad trade.

**Titan's Strength** (4/11) · **Bulk Up** (3/11) · **Brute Force** (2/11) · **Infuriate** (2/11) ·
**Reckless Charge** (2/11) · **Haste Magic** (3/11) · Brute Strength · Fists of the Anvil ·
Rush of Blood · Screaming Fury · Borrowed Hostility · Gimli's Fury · Downhill Charge

Kept: **Blazing Shoal** (free, and scales to +9 or more), **Monstrous Rage** and
**Blazing Crescendo** (both replace themselves), **Livaan** and **Cait Sith** (repeatable).

## Cut on principle: equipment voltron

Three of the sample decks are Thor equipment decks; their equipment bled into the Wanda lists.
We only want equipment that pays for itself immediately.

**Champion's Helm** (4/11) · **Buster Sword** (3/11) · **Hero's Blade** (2/11) ·
**Glamdring** (2/11) · **Nettlecyst** (2/11) · **Hammer of Nazahn** (2/11) ·
**Excalibur, Sword of Eden** (2/11) · **Sword of Wealth and Power** (2/11) · **Dueling Rapier** (2/11)

Kept: **Commander's Plate** (protection from W/U/B/G dodges most removal in the format),
**Blackblade Reforged** (+1/+1 per land, equip {3} on a legendary), **Swiftfoot Boots**.

## Cut: redundancy and small stuff

- **Pyretic Charge** (4/11) — discard hand, draw 4. We already run Will of the Jeskai, Wheel of
  Fortune, Reforge the Soul. Fourth wheel is too many.
- **Decaying Time Loop** (3/11) · **Snort** (3/11) — same reason.
- **Demand Answers** (3/11) · **Thrill of Possibility** (2/11) — MV 2, no discount, weak draw.
- **Sorceress's Schemes** (3/11) — regrowth we have twice over.
- **Overwhelming Victory** (3/11) — a combat trick in a deck with ~6 creatures.
- **Nibelheim Aflame** (3/11) — worse Chandra's Ignition (hits creatures only, not players).
- **Redirect Lightning** (3/11) — third redirect after Deflecting Swat and Bolt Bend.
- **Reliquary Tower** (3/11) — we empty our hand every turn.
- **Lightning Bolt** (2/11) · **Pyroblast** (2/11) · **Ricochet Trap** (2/11) — too small.
- **Young Pyromancer** (2/11) · **Ashling, Flame Dancer** (2/11) — token/prowess plans we don't have.
- **Ragavan, Nimble Pilferer** (2/11) — needs combat and it's expensive.
- **Cursed Mirror** · **Fire Diamond** · **Thought Vessel** · **Cavern of Souls** ·
  **Mines of Moria** · **Fire Nation Palace** · **Abstergo Entertainment** — beaten by what's in.
- **Tavern Brawler** (3/11) — a Background; it does pump the commander each upkeep, but only on
  *your* upkeep, so it never grows the discount mid-turn when it matters.
- **The Vision** (2/11) · **Hex Magic** (2/11) · **Flashback** (2/11) — flavour picks that
  underperform. Say the word if flavour matters more than power here.

## Not in any sample deck, but I added them anyway

Defend these too:

- **Chandra's Ignition** (3/11, but underplayed) — the on-theme finisher. A pumped Wanda deals her
  power to each opponent *and* each other creature.
- **Thor, God of Thunder** (5/11 — I initially missed it) — damage equal to each noncreature
  spell's mana value. In this deck that's 4–11 per spell.
- **Arcane Bombardment** — snowballing free copies off the graveyard.
- **Hexing Squelcher** — "spells you control can't be countered" is mono-red's counterspell.
- **Tyrite Sanctum** — repeatable +1/+1 counter on Wanda (permanent discount growth), and it can
  give her an indestructible counter, which is how you survive your own Blasphemous Act.
- **Underworld Breach**, **Lion's Eye Diamond**, the moxen — Bracket 4 only.

## Known tension to resolve in the pass

**Blasphemous Act kills your own commander.** Commander's Plate grants protection from W/U/B/G —
not red — so it does not save her. The outs are Tyrite Sanctum's indestructible counter, or cutting
the wipe. Worth deciding deliberately rather than discovering it mid-game.
