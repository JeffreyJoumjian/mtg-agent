# The turn-5 chain — how a "Bracket 3" Wanda ends the game on turn 5 — 2026-09-08

Prompted by a play report, not a card question: an opponent piloting a Wanda list built much like
ours had her at roughly 25 power by turn 5, cast several big X-spells in one turn, resolved
[Storm King's Thunder](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Storm+King's+Thunder%22) at that size,
"drew 21 instead of 7", and ended the game. Three Game Changers, no banking engines, "a bunch of
1-mana equipment and some spells".

**Investigation only. No list was changed.** Every oracle text below was re-read with
`bun run card` today; the rules are cited from `rules/sections/`; the numbers come from a script
(`chain.ts`, reproduced in §2) rather than hand arithmetic.

---

## 1. The mechanism — it is two cards we already run

[The Scarlet Witch](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22The+Scarlet+Witch%22): *"Instant and
sorcery spells you cast with mana value 4 or greater cost {X} less to cast, where X is The Scarlet
Witch's power."*

[Livaan, Cultist of Tiamat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Livaan%2C+Cultist+of+Tiamat%22):
*"Whenever you cast a noncreature spell, target creature gets +X/+0 until end of turn, where X is
that spell's mana value."*

Three rules make those two cards a recurrence:

- **CR 202.3e** — *"When calculating the mana value of an object with an {X} in its mana cost …
  X is treated as the number chosen for it while the object is on the stack."* Livaan's trigger
  resolves while the spell is still on the stack, so it reads the full MV, X included.
- **CR 601.2f** — reductions come off the total cost, generic only (LEDGER 2026-08-07). Wanda's
  power at the moment you cast is the discount.
- **CR 107.3a** — you choose X as you cast; the spell's generic part is whatever X you name.

So for any X-spell with `p` coloured pips and `k` generic symbols per point of X (Crackle k = 3,
everything else k = 1), with Wanda at power `W` and `r` other reducers (Ruby, Longshot):

```
X  = ⌊(W + r) / k⌋          costs exactly p red pips
MV = kX + p
W' = W + MV  ≈ 2W + r + p   (Livaan's trigger, before the spell even resolves)
```

**Every X-spell in the deck is a Wanda-doubler for the price of its coloured pips.** The next
X-spell starts from the doubled number. That is the whole trick: not more mana, not Bracket 4
cards — an exponential that lives in the commander's own text plus a 30-cent 3-drop.

[Storm King's Thunder](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Storm+King's+Thunder%22) is the best
first link because it is a doubler **and** a copier: X = W for {R}{R}{R}, Wanda → 2W + 3, and the
next instant or sorcery is copied W times. Copies are not cast (CR 707.10 — they keep X; LEDGER
2026-08-02), so Livaan doesn't fire on them and Wanda doesn't need to discount them.

### 1a. Doublers that don't need Livaan

Scryfall `id:r (t:instant or t:sorcery) (o:"gets +X/+0" or o:"gets +X/+X")` returns 15 cards.
Four of them are `{X}{R}: target creature gets +X/+0` — [Lunar Frenzy](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Lunar+Frenzy%22)
and [Frantic Confrontation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Frantic+Confrontation%22)
(identical: first strike + trample), [Enrage](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Enrage%22)
(plain), [Pedal to the Metal](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Pedal+to+the+Metal%22) (first
strike). Cast on Wanda at W: X = W + r for a single {R}, so **she doubles with no engine on the
board**; with Livaan out the trigger adds X + 1 on top, so she *triples*. These are the
redundancy for Livaan, who is otherwise one card in 99 (the only other "+MV on cast" cards in red,
[Erratic Cyclops](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Erratic+Cyclops%22) and
[Renegade Bull](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Renegade+Bull%22), pump only themselves).

[Bionic Blow](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Bionic+Blow%22) ({X}{R}{R}: *"Target creature
you control gets +X/+0 … Then it deals damage equal to its power to up to one other target
creature"*) is the same doubler for two pips, triples under Livaan, and kills a creature for her
full pumped power on the way. [Blazing Shoal](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Blazing+Shoal%22)
is the free version (X = MV of the exiled red card). [Unleash Fury](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Unleash+Fury%22)
and [Bulk Up](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Bulk+Up%22) double for {1}{R} without an X.

## 2. The simulation

Livaan on board (cast turn 4), five red sources, no ritual, Ruby not out. "Seed" is Wanda's power
before the first X-spell — base 2, or 2 plus whatever permanent pump is on her.

| Seed | SKT | Wanda after | Jaya's | Copies | Damage to **each** opponent | Red pips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | X=2, {R}{R}{R} | 7 | X=7, {R}{R} | 2 | 7 × 3 = **21** | 5 |
| 4 | X=4 | 11 | X=11 | 4 | 11 × 5 = **55** | 5 |
| 6 | X=6 | 15 | X=15 | 6 | 15 × 7 = **105** | 5 |
| 8 | X=8 | 19 | X=19 | 8 | 19 × 9 = **171** | 5 |
| 12 | X=12 | 27 | X=27 | 12 | 27 × 13 = **351** | 5 |

With Ruby Medallion out the seed-2 line is 36 each and seed 4 is 78 each. Substituting
[Crackle with Power](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Crackle+with+Power%22) (three generic per
X) for Jaya's: seed 2 → 30 to each of 2 targets, seed 4 → 75 to each of 3, seed 8 → 270 to each
of 6. With a ritual for two more pips, a three-link chain from seed 2
([Bionic Blow](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Bionic+Blow%22) → SKT → Jaya's) is 171 each.

**Read the seed column.** The difference between "nobody dies" (seed 2, 21 each) and "the table
is dead" (seed 4, 55 each) is **one Bonesplitter**. Permanent power is not "+2 mana off a spell";
in a chain it is multiplied by every link after it. That is what the opponent's cheap equipment
was doing, and it is why our own `considered-and-cut.md` grounds for cutting one-shot pump
(*"+2 or +3 power for one turn, roughly one extra discount — a bad trade"*) are wrong for this
commander. They were written before the recurrence was.

The refuel he did: [Repeated Reverberation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Repeated+Reverberation%22)
({R}{R} after Wanda) then [Commune with Lava](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Commune+with+Lava%22)
at X=7 for {R}{R} = three Communes = **21 cards** exiled, playable until the end of his next turn.
Or a wheel copied twice. Either is "instead of 7 he drew 21", and both are in V2.

What Wanda at 25 does, for completeness: SKT X=25 → 53; Jaya's X=53, copied 25 times → 1,378 to
each of three targets. The number doesn't matter; what matters is that it cost five red pips.

The script:

```ts
// chain.ts — bun run chain.ts
type Spell = { name: string; pips: number; k: number; fixed?: number };
const SKT = { name: "Storm King's Thunder", pips: 3, k: 1 };
const JAYA = { name: "Jaya's Immolating Inferno", pips: 2, k: 1 };
const CRACKLE = { name: "Crackle with Power", pips: 2, k: 3 };
function run(seed: number, r: number, chain: Spell[]) {
  let w = seed, copies = 0;
  for (const s of chain) {
    const x = s.fixed !== undefined ? 0 : Math.floor((w + r) / s.k);
    const mv = (s.fixed ?? s.k * x) + s.pips;
    const before = w; w += mv;                      // Livaan: +MV/+0
    console.log(s.name, `X=${x}`, `pay ${"R".repeat(s.pips)}`, `MV ${mv}`, `Wanda ${before}→${w}`,
      s === SKT ? `copies next ×${x}` : `× ${copies + 1} instances`);
    copies = s === SKT ? x : 0;
  }
}
for (const seed of [2, 4, 6, 8, 12]) run(seed, 0, [SKT, JAYA]);
```

## 5. Pip economics — the constraint changes

Once Wanda's power exceeds the next spell's generic, **that spell costs only its coloured pips**,
and nothing colourless can pay a pip. So on the chain turn:

- Total mana stops mattering; **red sources** are the budget. SKT → Jaya's is five red whether the
  chain deals 21 or 1,378. Adding Reverberation is seven.
- [Seething Song](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Seething+Song%22) ({2}{R} → {R}{R}{R}{R}{R})
  is **+4 red pips** when its {2} is paid with Sol Ring — the rituals are the chain's extra links,
  which is the same conclusion the pilot reached from the banking side (LEDGER 2026-08-21).
- [Sol Ring](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Sol+Ring%22), Nykthos' base ability, War Room,
  Rogue's Passage, Tyrite Sanctum and Forge of Heroes pay for Livaan's {2}, equip costs and
  rituals' generic. They contribute nothing to the chain itself.
- The per-spell engines re-price as **pip refunds**: [Electro](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Electro%2C+Assaulting+Battery%22)
  / [Urabrask](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Urabrask%22) / [The Vision and Scarlet Witch](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22The+Vision+and+Scarlet+Witch%22)
  give back one of the 2–3 pips per cast. [Storm-Kiln Artist](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Storm-Kiln+Artist%22)
  triggers on cast **or copy** — under SKT ×8 that is **ten** Treasures (the Thunder's own cast, the copied spell's cast, and eight copies), all red if you want. Ashling's
  magecraft also fires per copy: nine loots, the 2-damage sweep on the second, {R}{R}{R}{R} on the
  third. Those two are the engines that scale with the chain; [Neheb](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Neheb%2C+the+Eternal%22)
  (postcombat, needs damage first) is the one that doesn't.

## 6. Where the chain can be stopped, and what covers it

It is a sequence of casts on one turn. An opponent can respond to SKT, or to the Livaan trigger,
by removing Wanda or Livaan; with Wanda gone the next X is 0 + reducers.

- [Conqueror's Flail](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Conqueror's+Flail%22) attached to
  **any** creature: *"your opponents can't cast spells during your turn"* — the chain becomes
  interruptible only by activated abilities. This is the best card in the deck for this plan.
  Keep the 2026-08-19 note: attach it to something other than Wanda.
- [Deflecting Swat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Deflecting+Swat%22) (free with a commander)
  and [Return the Favor](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Return+the+Favor%22) for the ability
  or the spell that gets through.
- [Mithril Coat](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Mithril+Coat%22) / Tyrite Sanctum for the
  destroy row; Helm and Plate for targeted removal on other turns (LEDGER threat matrix
  2026-08-21).
- Equipment survives Wanda dying (CR 704.5n, LEDGER 2026-08-07) — a seed on the board is a seed
  again after the recast, which is why cheap-equip and auto-attach equipment beat one-shot pump
  for the *seed* role, while the `{X}{R}` instants beat equipment for the *doubler* role.

triple a turn that already deals hundreds — it is a fair-turn card in a chain deck, and that is a
real question for the table, not a verdict.

**Bracket.** The chain uses no Game Changer, is not an infinite (bounded by red pips and cards),
and is two cards we have run since 2026-07-01. It is a Bracket-3-legal deck that kills on turn 5
when the seed is there. Pods will experience it as Bracket 4, which is exactly what the pilot
saw from the other side. Their call how far to lean.
