# Arabella — Pilot's gameplan

## The one-paragraph version

Play small creatures and token makers, then attack with Arabella every turn. Each attack drains each
opponent for your creatures with power 2 or less and gains you that much. Delney and Windcrag Siege
each add a trigger, Kirol copies one, Torbran adds 2 to each, and City on Fire triples them. After
her triggers resolve, Reconnaissance takes her out of combat for {0} and untaps her, so she never
has to survive a block.

## Ordering the attack

1. Declare attackers. Arabella's trigger and every "whenever you attack" token maker trigger
   together (Adeline, Anim Pakal, Ainok Strike Leader, Voice of Victory, Myrel, Krenko).
2. Put Arabella's triggers on the stack **first**, so they resolve **last**. The tokens from the
   other triggers are on the battlefield by then and count (X is counted as each trigger resolves,
   CR 608.2h).
3. With Kirol, tap two untapped creatures to copy one of her triggers.
4. After her triggers resolve, use Reconnaissance on her if she might be blocked.

## Things that shrink X

- Anything that pushes a creature to power 3 before her triggers resolve: Legion Warboss's mentor
  counter on a 2-power creature, Purphoros's {2}{R} pump, Elspeth, Sun's Champion's emblem.
- Pumps after her triggers resolve are free: they no longer change X.
- Anim Pakal and Krenko grow past 2 after a couple of attacks.

## Your own wipes

Dusk // Dawn and The Battle of Bywater kill power 3 or more, so most of your board lives, but
Adeline, Myrel, Mondrak, Ojer Taq, Kirol, Aurelia, Combat Celebrant and Resplendent Angel die too.
Dawn (from the graveyard) returns every creature card with power 2 or less to your hand.

## Damage math

- Torbran adds 2 to each red source's damage to each opponent: Arabella, Impact Tremors, Purphoros,
  Molten Gatekeeper, Agate Instigator and Raid Bombardment.
- With Torbran and City on Fire both out, the damaged player orders them (CR 616.1), so expect
  X × 3 + 2, not (X + 2) × 3.
- City on Fire also triples the damage your Talisman of Conviction and Battlefield Forge deal to you.
